# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A loan of $700,000 is secured by assets worth $1,000,000. What is the loan-to-value ratio?
   - [x] 70%
   - 30%
   - 143%
   - 7%
   > Loan-to-value = $700,000 / $1,000,000 = 70%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A lender requires loan-to-value to stay at or below 80%. A $600,000 loan is secured by assets that fall to $700,000. How much must the borrower repay to get back to 80%?
   - $100,000
   - $50,000
   - [x] $40,000
   - $0, since the loan is under the collateral value
   > At 80%, $700,000 of collateral supports a loan of at most $560,000. The borrower must repay $600,000 − $560,000 = $40,000.
