# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A taxable bond pays 6% interest and the investor's tax rate is 30%. What is the after-tax yield?
   - 1.8%
   - 3.6%
   - 6%
   - [x] 4.2%
   > After-tax yield = 6% × (1 − 0.30) = 4.2%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] An investor receives $2,000 of qualified dividends taxed at 15%. How much do they keep after tax?
   - $300
   - [x] $1,700
   - $1,850
   - $2,000
   > Tax = $2,000 × 15% = $300, so the investor keeps $2,000 − $300 = $1,700.

3. {#bk3} [calc] An investor buys a stock for $10,000 and sells it for $14,000 after two years. Long-term gains are taxed at 20%. What is the after-tax gain?
   - $4,000
   - $800
   - $2,800
   - [x] $3,200
   > The gain is $4,000. Tax is 20% × $4,000 = $800. After-tax gain = $3,200.

4. {#bk4} [calc] An investor receives $5,000 of interest taxed as ordinary income at 32%. How much do they keep after tax?
   - [x] $3,400
   - $1,600
   - $4,680
   - $5,000
   > Tax = 32% × $5,000 = $1,600. After tax = $3,400.
