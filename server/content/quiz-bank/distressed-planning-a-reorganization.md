# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A company produces EBITDA of $20 million and comparable firms trade at 6 times EBITDA. What is its estimated enterprise value?
   - $3.3 million
   - [x] $120 million
   - $26 million
   - $14 million
   > Enterprise value = EBITDA × multiple = $20 million × 6 = $120 million.

2. {#bk2} [calc] In a reorganization, creditors receive 80% of the new equity, valued at $90 million. What is the value of the creditors' share?
   - $90 million
   - $18 million
   - $112 million
   - [x] $72 million
   > 80% × $90 million = $72 million.
