# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trend-following rule is applied to 6 ETFs from different asset classes. 4 are in uptrends and the rule holds each uptrend at an equal 1/6 weight, with the rest in cash. How much of the portfolio is invested?
   - 100%
   - 33.3%
   - [x] 66.7%
   - 16.7%
   > 4 positions × 1/6 = 66.7% invested, with 33.3% in cash.

2. {#bk2} [calc] Four uncorrelated ETF trend positions each have a volatility of 12% and are equally weighted. What is the volatility of the portfolio?
   - [x] 6%
   - 12%
   - 3%
   - 48%
   > With uncorrelated equal weights, volatility falls with the square root of the number of positions: 12% / √4 = 6%.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A multi-asset trend rule sizes each ETF by target volatility divided by its own volatility. The target is 10% and an ETF has a volatility of 20%. What weight does it get?
   - 200%
   - 10%
   - [x] 50%
   - 20%
   > Weight = 10% / 20% = 50%.
