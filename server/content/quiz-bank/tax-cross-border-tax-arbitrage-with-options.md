# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An investor holds 100,000 shares paying a $2 dividend with 30% withholding. A hedge structure reclaims 15% of the dividend at an option cost of $12,000. What is the net benefit?
   - $30,000
   - $42,000
   - $12,000
   - [x] $18,000
   > The dividend is $200,000. The 15% reclaimed is $30,000. Net = $30,000 − $12,000 = $18,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] An investor receives a $2 dividend on 100,000 shares and 30% is withheld. How much cash do they receive before any reclaim?
   - $60,000
   - [x] $140,000
   - $200,000
   - $170,000
   > Dividend = $200,000. Withholding = $60,000. Cash received = $140,000.
