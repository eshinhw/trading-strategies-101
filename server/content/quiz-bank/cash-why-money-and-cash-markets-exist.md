# Quiz

1. {#q1} Which of these is one of the three jobs of money?
   - [x] A medium of exchange, so people don't have to barter
   - A guarantee of profit
   - A source of unlimited credit
   - A replacement for taxes
   > Money works as a medium of exchange, a store of value, and a unit of account.

2. {#q2} Why do organizations have uneven cash needs?
   - Because cash is always spent immediately
   - [x] Because cash comes in and goes out at different times, leaving some with surplus cash and others short
   - Because banks never hold cash
   - Because governments never spend
   > Timing mismatches mean some hold more cash than they need while others need to borrow.

3. {#q3} What do short-term cash markets do?
   - They set the price of stocks
   - [x] They match organizations with surplus cash with those that need to borrow for a short time
   - They issue long-term bonds only
   - They remove the need for banks
   > Money markets let lenders earn a modest return and borrowers obtain funding quickly.

4. {#q4} For a cash investor, which priorities come first?
   - Maximum yield above all
   - [x] Not losing the principal and being able to get the money back when needed
   - Taking as much risk as possible
   - Long maturities
   > Safety and liquidity come before yield for cash held for short periods.

5. {#q5} A manufacturer has cash now but owes large supplier payments in June. Why does it buy short-term Treasury bills in the meantime?
   - To speculate on stocks
   - [x] To earn a small return with almost no risk until the cash is needed in June
   - To avoid paying suppliers
   - To buy a factory
   > Bills that mature before the payments are due keep the money safe and accessible while earning a little.

6. {#calc1} [calc] A manufacturer has $8 million idle for 90 days and buys a Treasury bill yielding 5% a year (360-day basis). About how much does it earn?
   - About $400,000
   - About $25,000
   - [x] About $100,000
   - About $10,000
   > Interest is $8,000,000 × 5% × 90/360 = $100,000.

7. {#calc2} [calc] A bank borrows $200 million overnight at 5.1% a year (360-day basis). What does the night cost?
   - [x] About $28,333
   - About $10,200,000
   - About $283,333
   - About $1,020
   > $200,000,000 × 5.1% / 360 ≈ $28,333.
