# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader buys 100,000 of the base currency in EUR/USD at 1.0850, and it rises to 1.0912. What is the move in pips and the profit in dollars?
   - +620 pips, +$6,200
   - 62 pips, $620
   - [x] +62 pips, +$620
   - +62 pips, +$62
   > The move is 1.0912 − 1.0850 = +0.0062, or +62 pips. On 100,000 units: +0.0062 × 100,000 = +$620.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader buys 200,000 of the base currency in GBP/USD at 1.2600, and it rises to 1.2540. What is the move in pips and the profit in dollars?
   - [x] −60 pips, −$1,200
   - −600 pips, −$12,000
   - 60 pips, $1,200
   - −60 pips, −$120
   > The move is 1.2540 − 1.2600 = −0.0060, or −60 pips. On 200,000 units: −0.0060 × 200,000 = −$1,200.

3. {#bk3} [calc] USD/JPY moves from 148.50 to 149.20. A pip is 0.01 yen. How many pips is that?
   - 7 pips
   - 700 pips
   - [x] 70 pips
   - 0.7 pips
   > The move is 149.20 − 148.50 = 0.70 yen, which is 70 pips of 0.01.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A trader holds 100,000 units of EUR/USD. What is one pip (0.0001) worth in dollars?
   - $1
   - [x] $10
   - $100
   - $0.10
   > One pip is 0.0001 × 100,000 = $10.

5. {#bk5} [calc] EUR/USD is quoted at 1.0800. How many dollars does it take to buy €50,000?
   - €46,296
   - $46,296
   - $50,000
   - [x] $54,000
   > Each euro costs $1.0800, so €50,000 costs 50,000 × 1.0800 = $54,000.
