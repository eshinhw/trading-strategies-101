# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A landlord charges $24,000 a year in rent and rents rise with 3% inflation. What is next year's rent?
   - $24,072
   - $27,000
   - [x] $24,720
   - $23,280
   > $24,000 × 1.03 = $24,720.

2. {#bk2} [calc] Rent of $30,000 is indexed to inflation with a cap of 3% a year. Inflation is 4%. What is the new rent?
   - [x] $30,900
   - $31,200
   - $30,000
   - $33,000
   > The cap limits the rise to 3%: $30,000 × 1.03 = $30,900.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A property earns a 5% net rental yield and its rent grows with 3% inflation. Excluding any change in value, what is the approximate yield on cost next year?
   - 5.0%
   - 8.0%
   - [x] 5.15%
   - 3.0%
   > Rent rises 3%, so the yield rises 5% × 1.03 = 5.15%.
