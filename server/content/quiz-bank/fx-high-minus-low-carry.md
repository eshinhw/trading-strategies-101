# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A high-minus-low carry strategy buys the high-yielding currencies, averaging 6%, and sells the low-yielding ones, averaging 1%, with $2 million on each side. What is the yearly interest carry?
   - [x] $100,000
   - $140,000
   - $10,000
   - $200,000
   > The long side earns 6% × $2 million = $120,000. The short side costs 1% × $2 million = $20,000. Net = $100,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A high-minus-low carry basket earns 5% of carry a year, but the high-yield currencies fall 3% on average against the low-yield ones. What is the net return?
   - 8%
   - 5%
   - [x] 2%
   - −3%
   > Net = 5% carry − 3% currency loss = 2%.
