# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a Long Call: buys one $100 call, for a net debit of $4 per share. What is the break-even stock price at expiration, per share?
   - $4
   - [x] $104
   - $105
   - $103
   > For this position (K = 100, net debit = 4), the break-even stock price is $104 per share. Formula: S* = K + D.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader builds a Long Call: buys one $100 call, for a net debit of $4 per share. At expiration the stock is at $103. What is the trader's profit or loss per share, counting the premium?
   - +$1
   - +$3
   - +$7
   - [x] −$1
   > At $103 the option legs are worth +$3 per share before the premium, and the net premium adds −$4, for −$1 per share (given K = 100, net debit = 4).

4. {#bk4} [calc] A trader builds a Long Call: buys one $100 call, for a net debit of $4 per share. What is the maximum loss on 8 contracts (100 shares each), in dollars?
   - $32
   - $400
   - [x] $3,200
   - $3,600
   > The maximum loss is $4 per share. One contract covers 100 shares, so 8 contracts give $4 × 100 × 8 = $3,200.
