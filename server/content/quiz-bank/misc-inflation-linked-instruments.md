# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A TIPS has a $1,000 principal and a 1.5% real coupon. Inflation adjusts the principal to $1,040. What is the annual coupon?
   - [x] $15.60
   - $15.00
   - $16.00
   - $1.56
   > The coupon is paid on the adjusted principal: 1.5% × $1,040 = $15.60.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A 5-year nominal bond yields 4.0% while a 5-year TIPS yields 1.8%. If inflation averages 2.5%, which would perform better, and by about how much per year?
   - The nominal bond, by about 0.3%
   - The TIPS, by about 2.2%
   - [x] The TIPS, by about 0.3%
   - They would perform equally
   > The breakeven is 4.0% − 1.8% = 2.2%. Inflation of 2.5% is 0.3% higher than that, so the TIPS beats the nominal bond by about 0.3% a year.
