# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A forward agreement requires collateral for exposure above a $500,000 threshold. The forward's mark-to-market is $1,200,000 in favour of one party. How much collateral must the other side post?
   - $1,200,000
   - $500,000
   - $1,700,000
   - [x] $700,000
   > Collateral covers the exposure above the threshold: $1,200,000 − $500,000 = $700,000.
