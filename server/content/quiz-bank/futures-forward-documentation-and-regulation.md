# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A forward agreement requires collateral for exposure above a $500,000 threshold. The forward's mark-to-market is $1,200,000 in favour of one party. How much collateral must the other side post?
   - $1,200,000
   - $500,000
   - $1,700,000
   - [x] $700,000
   > Collateral covers the exposure above the threshold: $1,200,000 − $500,000 = $700,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trade confirmation shows 25,000 bushels of wheat at a forward price of $6.45, physically settled. What payment is made at settlement?
   - $6.45
   - [x] $161,250
   - $16,125
   - $1,612,500
   > Payment = 25,000 × $6.45 = $161,250.

3. {#bk3} [calc] Collateral is exchanged as a forward's value changes. The mark-to-market in a party's favour rises from $1.2 million to $1.9 million. How much additional collateral does the other side owe (ignoring thresholds)?
   - $1,900,000
   - $3,100,000
   - $70,000
   - [x] $700,000
   > $1,900,000 − $1,200,000 = $700,000.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A cash-settled currency forward has a notional of €5,000,000 at 1.0700. At maturity the spot rate is 1.0850 and the buyer is long euros. How much does the buyer receive?
   - [x] $75,000
   - $7,500
   - $750,000
   - $5,425,000
   > (1.0850 − 1.0700) × €5,000,000 = $75,000.

5. {#bk5} What is the purpose of a trade confirmation?
   - It guarantees the counterparty will pay
   - It sets the exchange's daily price limit
   - [x] It records the exact agreed terms and is the definitive record of the deal
   - It replaces the need for a forward price
   > With no exchange trade ticket to fall back on, the written confirmation shows the asset, notional amount, delivery date, forward price and settlement method.

6. {#bk6} What does an ISDA Master Agreement do?
   - [x] Covers the standard legal terms for all future OTC trades between two repeat counterparties
   - Replaces the clearinghouse
   - Fixes the forward price of every trade
   - Sets the interest rate on each deal
   > Counterparties sign one master agreement and then only need a short confirmation for each trade.

7. {#bk7} What did post-2008 reforms require for many OTC derivatives, including forwards?
   - Moving all trades onto exchanges
   - A ban on cash settlement
   - [x] Reporting to trade repositories and regulatory collateral for certain counterparties
   - Fixed prices set by regulators
   > The reforms extended some of the transparency and collateral features of exchange trading to the OTC market.
