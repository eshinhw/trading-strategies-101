# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A dollar carry rule goes long a basket of foreign currencies when the US interest rate is below the average foreign rate, and long the dollar otherwise. US rates are 5% and the average foreign rate is 3.5%. Which position does it take, and what is the yearly carry on $10 million?
   - Long foreign currencies, carry $150,000
   - [x] Long the dollar, carry $150,000
   - Long the dollar, carry $1.5 million
   - Long foreign currencies, carry $1.5 million
   > US rates are above the foreign average, so the rule is long the dollar. Carry = (5% − 3.5%) × $10 million = $150,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader is long the dollar against a foreign basket on $10 million, earning 1.5% in carry. The foreign basket appreciates 2% against the dollar. What is the net result?
   - +$350,000
   - −$200,000
   - +$150,000
   - [x] −$50,000
   > Carry = +$150,000. The currency loss = 2% × $10 million = $200,000. Net = −$50,000.

3. {#bk3} [calc] The US interest rate is 2% and the average foreign rate is 4.5%. A dollar carry rule goes long a foreign basket funded in dollars. What yearly carry does it earn on $5 million?
   - $75,000
   - [x] $125,000
   - $325,000
   - $12,500
   > Carry = (4.5% − 2%) × $5 million = 2.5% × $5 million = $125,000.
