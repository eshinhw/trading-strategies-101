# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A stock is offered at $50 per share in cash and trades at $48. What is the gross spread as a percentage of the current price?
   - [x] 4.17%
   - 4.0%
   - 2.0%
   - 8.33%
   > The spread is $50 − $48 = $2. As a percentage of $48 it is 4.17%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A merger-arbitrage position earns a 4.17% spread and the deal is expected to close in 6 months. What is the simple annualized return?
   - 4.17%
   - 2.08%
   - [x] 8.33%
   - 16.7%
   > 4.17% × (12 / 6) = 8.33% on a simple basis.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A target trades at $48 against a $50 offer. If the deal breaks the stock falls to $38. What deal-completion probability is implied by the price?
   - [x] 83.3%
   - 96%
   - 50%
   - 76%
   > $48 = p × $50 + (1 − p) × $38 gives 48 − 38 = p × 12, so p = 10 / 12 = 83.3%.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A target's shareholders receive 0.8 shares of the acquirer for each target share. The acquirer trades at $50 and the target at $38. What is the gross spread per target share?
   - $12
   - $0.80
   - $40
   - [x] $2
   > The offer is worth 0.8 × $50 = $40. The spread is $40 − $38 = $2.

5. {#bk5} [calc] In a stock-for-stock deal each target share converts into 0.8 acquirer shares. A trader buys 10,000 target shares. How many acquirer shares should be sold short to hedge?
   - 10,000
   - [x] 8,000
   - 12,500
   - 800
   > Hedge ratio = 0.8 per target share, so 10,000 × 0.8 = 8,000 acquirer shares.

6. {#bk6} [calc] A merger-arbitrage trader earns $2 on a $38 target if the deal closes, and loses $9 if it collapses. What probability of completion is needed to break even?
   - 50%
   - 18.2%
   - 95%
   - [x] 81.8%
   > Break even: p × $2 = (1 − p) × $9, so p = 9 / 11 = 81.8%.

7. {#bk7} Why do merger-arbitrage returns resemble selling insurance?
   - Gains are large and uncapped when a deal closes
   - [x] Gains are small and capped when a deal closes, while a collapsed deal causes a comparatively large, sudden loss
   - The strategy has no downside
   - Returns do not depend on the deal completing
   > The target can only converge up to the deal price, while a failed deal can drop it a long way.
