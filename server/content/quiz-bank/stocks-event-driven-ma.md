# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A stock is offered at $50 per share in cash and trades at $48. What is the gross spread as a percentage of the current price?
   - [x] 4.17%
   - 4.0%
   - 2.0%
   - 8.33%
   > The spread is $50 − $48 = $2. As a percentage of $48 it is 4.17%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A merger-arbitrage position earns a 4.17% spread and the deal is expected to close in 6 months. What is the simple annualized return?
   - 4.17%
   - 2.08%
   - [x] 8.33%
   - 16.7%
   > 4.17% × (12 / 6) = 8.33% on a simple basis.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A target trades at $48 against a $50 offer. If the deal breaks the stock falls to $38. What deal-completion probability is implied by the price?
   - [x] 83.3%
   - 96%
   - 50%
   - 76%
   > $48 = p × $50 + (1 − p) × $38 gives 48 − 38 = p × 12, so p = 10 / 12 = 83.3%.
