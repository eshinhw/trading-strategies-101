# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A futures price is 82, its 20-day average is 75, and the standard deviation is 3.5. What is the z-score?
   - 7.0
   - 0.5
   - [x] 2.0
   - 2.3
   > z = (price − average) / standard deviation = (82 − 75) / 3.5 = 2.0.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A contrarian trader sells one contract (1,000 units per contract) at 82, expecting a return to the 75 average. What is the profit if the price gets there?
   - [x] $7,000
   - $3,500
   - $82,000
   - $70,000
   > Profit = (82 − 75) × 1,000 = $7,000.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A contrarian trader uses bands at the 20-day average plus or minus 2 standard deviations. The average is 75 and the standard deviation is 3.5. At what price does a sell signal appear?
   - Above 78.5
   - Above 85.5
   - [x] Above 82
   - Above 75
   > The upper band is 75 + 2 × 3.5 = 82, so prices above 82 trigger a short.
