# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A firm has $2 million of idle cash that earns 4% (365-day basis) in a sweep account for 90 days. What interest does it earn?
   - $20,000
   - [x] $19,726
   - $80,000
   - $6,575
   > Interest = $2,000,000 × 4% × 90 / 365 = $19,726.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A company has $1.5 million of current assets and $1.0 million of current liabilities. What is its current ratio?
   - 0.67
   - 0.5
   - 2.5
   - [x] 1.5
   > Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities = $1.5 million / $1.0 million = 1.5.
