# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] After novation a trade of 100 contracts between buyer A and seller B is replaced by two contracts with the clearinghouse. What is the clearinghouse's net position?
   - Long 100 contracts
   - Short 100 contracts
   - Long 200 contracts
   - [x] Zero, because it is long 100 to B and short 100 to A
   > The clearinghouse becomes the seller to A and the buyer from B in equal size, so its net market exposure is zero.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A clearing member's customers hold 30 long and 20 short contracts of the same future. The clearinghouse charges $5,000 per contract on the net position. What is the margin?
   - $250,000
   - [x] $50,000
   - $150,000
   - $100,000
   > The net position is 30 − 20 = 10 contracts. 10 × $5,000 = $50,000.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A clearing member defaults with a $12 million loss. Its own margin of $5 million is used first, then the $20 million guarantee fund. How much is left in the guarantee fund?
   - $8 million
   - $15 million
   - $20 million
   - [x] $13 million
   > The member's margin covers $5 million. The remaining $7 million comes from the fund, leaving $20 million − $7 million = $13 million.

4. {#bk4} [calc] Crude oil falls $1.20 in a day. Traders who are long 10 contracts (1,000 barrels each) are settled by the clearinghouse. How much do those longs pay in total that day?
   - [x] $12,000
   - $1,200
   - $120,000
   - $10,000
   > Loss = $1.20 × 1,000 × 10 = $12,000, paid in cash through daily settlement.

5. {#bk5} What does novation do to a futures trade?
   - It cancels the trade and returns margin to both traders
   - It makes the buyer and seller guarantee each other's performance
   - [x] It replaces the original contract with two: one between the buyer and the clearinghouse, one between the clearinghouse and the seller
   - It converts the futures contract into a forward contract
   > After novation each trader's counterparty is the clearinghouse, so no one needs to assess the creditworthiness of whoever was on the other side.

6. {#bk6} How does the clearinghouse keep its exposure small?
   - [x] Daily mark-to-market and margin calls mean it is rarely exposed to more than about a day's adverse move
   - It lends its own money to traders who lose
   - It only accepts trades from members with perfect credit ratings
   - It limits every trader to one contract
   > Because gains and losses are collected in cash every day, a loss cannot build up unpaid for long.

7. {#bk7} What is the guarantee fund at a clearinghouse?
   - A fund paid for by the exchange to give traders profits
   - A fund the defaulting member alone contributes to after it fails
   - [x] A pool contributed to by all clearing members that absorbs losses when a defaulting member's margin is not enough
   - A fund used only to pay brokers' commissions
   > The mutualized fund is a backstop behind each member's own margin and the daily margining process.
