# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A commodity's spot price is $78 and its three-month future is $81. What is the basis (spot minus futures), and which curve shape is that?
   - $3, backwardation
   - −$3, backwardation
   - [x] −$3, contango
   - $3, contango
   > The basis is $78 − $81 = −$3. A futures price above spot (negative basis) is contango, and below spot (positive basis) is backwardation, so this is contango.

7. {#bk7} [calc] A farmer is short 40,000 bushels of corn futures as a hedge. The basis strengthens from −$0.30 to −$0.10. What does the change in basis add to the hedged result?

   > The basis improves by $0.20 per bushel. A short hedger gains when the basis strengthens: $0.20 × 40,000 = $8,000.
