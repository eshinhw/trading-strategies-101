# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A Treasury bill with a $100 face value is bought at 99.00 and matures in 180 days. What is its annualized yield (365-day basis, simple)?
   - 2.00%
   - [x] 2.05%
   - 1.00%
   - 1.01%
   > Return over 180 days = (100 − 99.00) / 99.00 = 1.010%. Annualized: × 365/180 = 2.05%.
