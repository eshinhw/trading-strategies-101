# Quiz

1. {#q1} Where do most bonds trade?
   - On a single central exchange
   - [x] Over the counter through dealers who quote bids and asks
   - Only directly with the issuer
   - Only through banks' branches
   > Most bonds trade in negotiated, dealer-based markets rather than on an exchange.

2. {#q2} [calc] A bond is quoted at 98.50 and has a face value of 1,000 dollars. What is the clean price?
   - 98.50 dollars
   - 1,098.50 dollars
   - [x] 985 dollars
   - 1,000 dollars
   > A quote of 98.50 means 98.5% of face value, which is 985 dollars on a 1,000 dollar bond.

3. {#q3} What is accrued interest?
   - A fee the dealer charges
   - Interest the bond will earn after maturity
   - [x] Interest earned since the last coupon payment, which the buyer pays the seller
   - The bond's face value
   > Coupons are paid periodically but interest builds up daily, so a buyer compensates the seller for the interest earned while the seller held the bond.

4. {#q4} How is the dirty price related to the clean price?
   - Dirty price equals clean price minus accrued interest
   - They are always equal
   - [x] Dirty price equals clean price plus accrued interest
   - Dirty price is the face value
   > The dirty price is the quoted clean price plus the interest accrued since the last coupon, which is the amount actually paid.

5. {#q5} [calc] A bond pays a 30 dollar coupon each period, and 60 of 180 days have passed. What is the accrued interest?
   - 30 dollars
   - 60 dollars
   - [x] 10 dollars
   - 5 dollars
   > Accrued interest = 30 × 60 / 180 = 10 dollars.

6. {#q6} [calc] A bond with a $1,000 face value and a 5% coupon (paid twice a year) is quoted at 98.50, and it is 60 days into a 180-day coupon period. What is the dirty price per bond?
   - $985.00
   - $991.67
   - [x] $993.33
   - $1,000.00
   > The clean price is $985, and the accrued interest is $25 × 60 / 180 = $8.33, so the dirty price is $993.33.

7. {#q7} An investor buys 100 of those bonds at the dirty price. What do they pay in total?
   - $98,500
   - [x] $99,333
   - $100,000
   - $101,333
   > 100 bonds at $993.33 each is $99,333, which is the $98,500 clean price plus $833.33 of accrued interest owed to the seller.
