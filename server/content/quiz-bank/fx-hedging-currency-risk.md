# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A US exporter will receive €3 million in 6 months. Spot is 1.1000 and the 6-month forward is 1.1200. If the exporter hedges and the spot rate is 1.0500 when payment arrives, how many dollars does it receive?
   - $3,150,000
   - $3,300,000
   - $3,450,000
   - [x] $3,360,000
   > The forward locks in 1.1200: €3,000,000 × 1.1200 = $3,360,000, no matter where the spot rate ends.
