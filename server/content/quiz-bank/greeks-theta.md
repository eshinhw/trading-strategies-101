# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An option has a theta of −$0.05 per share per day. A trader holds 5 contracts (100 shares each) for 10 days with no other change. What is the time-decay loss?
   - [x] $250
   - $25
   - $2.50
   - $500
   > Decay = $0.05 × 100 shares × 5 contracts × 10 days = $250.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A short option position earns $0.04 per share a day from time decay. What does the trader earn on 100 shares over 7 days?
   - $4
   - $280
   - [x] $28
   - $0.28
   > Earnings = $0.04 × 100 × 7 = $28.
