# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A flipper buys a house for $200,000, spends $50,000 on repairs, pays $30,000 in holding and selling costs, and sells for $330,000. What is the profit?
   - $80,000
   - $130,000
   - [x] $50,000
   - $100,000
   > Total costs are $200,000 + $50,000 + $30,000 = $280,000. Profit = $330,000 − $280,000 = $50,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] Using the 70% rule, a house will be worth $300,000 after repairs, which cost $40,000. What is the maximum purchase price?
   - [x] $170,000
   - $210,000
   - $260,000
   - $130,000
   > Maximum offer = 70% × $300,000 − $40,000 = $210,000 − $40,000 = $170,000.
