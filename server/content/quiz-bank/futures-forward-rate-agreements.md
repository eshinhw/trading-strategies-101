# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A company buys a FRA on $50,000,000 at 5% for a 6-month period. At settlement the reference rate is 5.75%. What is the payment to the company, discounted to the start of the period?
   - [x] $182,260
   - $187,500
   - −$182,260
   - $375,000
   > $50,000,000 × (5.75% − 5%) × 0.5 = $187,500, divided by 1 + 5.75% × 0.5 = $182,260.
