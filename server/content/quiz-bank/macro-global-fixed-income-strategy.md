# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A foreign 10-year bond yields 5.0% and it costs 1.2% a year to hedge the currency back to dollars. A US 10-year yields 4.0%. What is the hedged pickup over the US bond?
   - [x] −0.2%, a hedged shortfall
   - +1.0%
   - +3.8%
   - +0.2%
   > The hedged yield is 5.0% − 1.2% = 3.8%. Compared with 4.0%, that is a shortfall of 0.2%.
