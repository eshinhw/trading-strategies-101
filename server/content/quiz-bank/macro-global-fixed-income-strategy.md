# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A foreign 10-year bond yields 5.0% and it costs 1.2% a year to hedge the currency back to dollars. A US 10-year yields 4.0%. What is the hedged pickup over the US bond?
   - [x] −0.2%, a hedged shortfall
   - +1.0%
   - +3.8%
   - +0.2%
   > The hedged yield is 5.0% − 1.2% = 3.8%. Compared with 4.0%, that is a shortfall of 0.2%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader is long a 10-year gilt yielding 4.30% and short a 10-year Bund yielding 2.60% with equal DV01 of $9,000 per basis point. The yield spread narrows by 12 basis points in the trade's favour. What is the profit?
   - $9,000
   - $1,080,000
   - [x] $108,000
   - $15,300
   > The spread starts at 170 basis points. The gain is $9,000 × 12 = $108,000.
