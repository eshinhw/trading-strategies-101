# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A utility loses $150,000 in revenue for each 100 HDDs below normal. A mild winter has 250 HDDs below normal. What is the revenue loss?
   - [x] $375,000
   - $150,000
   - $250,000
   - $600,000
   > Loss = 250 / 100 × $150,000 = $375,000.
