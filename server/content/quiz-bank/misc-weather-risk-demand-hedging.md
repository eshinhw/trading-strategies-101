# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A utility loses $150,000 in revenue for each 100 HDDs below normal. A mild winter has 250 HDDs below normal. What is the revenue loss?
   - [x] $375,000
   - $150,000
   - $250,000
   - $600,000
   > Loss = 250 / 100 × $150,000 = $375,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A utility bought a weather swap that pays it $25 for every heating degree day below 500, to offset lost heating revenue in a mild winter. The season records 430 HDDs. What happens, and how much?
   - [x] The utility receives $1,750
   - The utility pays $17,500
   - The utility pays $1,750
   - The utility receives $10,750
   > The shortfall is 500 − 430 = 70 HDDs. At $25 each the swap pays the utility $1,750.
