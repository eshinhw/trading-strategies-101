# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A low-volatility stock returns 8% with 12% volatility, and a high-volatility stock returns 9% with 30% volatility. The risk-free rate is 2%. What is each stock's Sharpe ratio?
   - [x] 0.50 for the low-volatility stock and 0.23 for the high-volatility stock
   - 0.67 and 0.30
   - 0.50 and 0.30
   - 0.23 for the low-volatility stock and 0.50 for the high-volatility stock
   > Sharpe = (return − risk-free) / volatility: (8 − 2) / 12 = 0.50 and (9 − 2) / 30 = 0.23.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A portfolio weights three stocks in proportion to the inverse of their volatilities: 10%, 20% and 40%. What weight does the lowest-volatility stock receive?
   - 33.3%
   - 71.4%
   - [x] 57.1%
   - 14.3%
   > Inverse volatilities are 10, 5 and 2.5, which sum to 17.5. The first weight is 10 / 17.5 = 57.1%.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A low-volatility fund loses 12% when the market falls 20%. What is the fund's downside capture?
   - [x] 60%
   - 12%
   - 40%
   - 167%
   > Downside capture = fund return / market return = −12% / −20% = 60%.
