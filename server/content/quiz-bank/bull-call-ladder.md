# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a Bull Call Ladder: buys one $100 call, sells one $105 call, and sells one $110 call, for no net premium. What is the maximum profit on 3 contracts (100 shares each), in dollars?
   - $15
   - [x] $1,500
   - $500
   - $2,000
   > The maximum profit is $5 per share. One contract covers 100 shares, so 3 contracts give $5 × 100 × 3 = $1,500.
