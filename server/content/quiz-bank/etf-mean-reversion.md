# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An ETF's 20-day average is $100 with a standard deviation of $2. It closes at $95.60. How many standard deviations below the average is it?
   - 4.4
   - 1.1
   - [x] 2.2
   - 2.0
   > ($100 − $95.60) / $2 = 2.2 standard deviations below the mean.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader buys an ETF at 95 expecting a return to 100, with a stop at 92. What is the reward-to-risk ratio?
   - [x] 1.67 to 1
   - 0.6 to 1
   - 3 to 1
   - 5 to 1
   > Reward = 100 − 95 = 5. Risk = 95 − 92 = 3. 5 / 3 = 1.67.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A trader buys 500 shares of an ETF at 95. The ETF reverts to 100. What is the profit?
   - $5
   - $47,500
   - [x] $2,500
   - $25,000
   > Profit = (100 − 95) × 500 = $2,500.
