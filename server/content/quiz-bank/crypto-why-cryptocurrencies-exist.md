# Quiz

1. {#q1} What problem was Bitcoin proposed to address?
   - [x] The need to trust a bank or other middleman to keep records and prevent double spending
   - The lack of any stock markets
   - The cost of printing banknotes
   - The shortage of gold
   > Bitcoin offered electronic cash that works without a central record keeper.

2. {#q2} How does a blockchain differ from a traditional ledger?
   - It is kept by a single bank
   - [x] Many computers keep identical copies and agree on valid transactions using shared rules
   - It can be changed by anyone at any time
   - It has no records
   > Consensus across many participants replaces a single trusted record keeper.

3. {#q3} What can smart contracts do?
   - Guarantee a profit
   - Replace all laws
   - [x] Run programs on a shared ledger that carry out agreed terms automatically
   - Print money
   > Smart contracts are programs on a blockchain, which Ethereum made widely available.

4. {#q4} Which is a common criticism of cryptocurrencies?
   - They have no price swings
   - [x] Extreme price volatility and the lack of cash flows behind most coins
   - They are impossible to buy
   - They are always backed by gold
   > Critics point to volatility, no underlying income, and misuse for scams and illicit finance.

5. {#q5} When sending money abroad, what is a trade-off of using cryptocurrency?
   - It is always more expensive
   - [x] It can be faster and avoid bank fees, but the coin's price may move sharply and conversion is needed
   - It cannot be sent across borders
   - The coin's price never changes
   > The technology can speed up transfers, but price volatility and conversion costs add new risks.

6. {#calc1} [calc] A worker sends $400 home. The bank route costs $44 in total. The crypto route has a $2 network fee plus a 1% conversion cost each way. How much cheaper is crypto, before price risk?
   - [x] $34
   - $42
   - $10
   - $44
   > The conversion cost is 2% × $400 = $8, so crypto costs $2 + $8 = $10 against $44 for the bank, which is $34 cheaper.

7. {#calc2} [calc] A worker sends $800. The bank route costs 11% and the crypto route costs 2.4%. How much cheaper is crypto?
   - $8.60
   - $88.00
   - [x] $68.80
   - $19.20
   > The bank costs 11% × $800 = $88.00 and crypto costs 2.4% × $800 = $19.20, so crypto is $68.80 cheaper (before price risk).
