# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A distressed bond has a 50% chance of recovering 80 cents and a 50% chance of recovering 20 cents. What is the expected recovery?
   - 80 cents
   - [x] 50 cents
   - 100 cents
   - 30 cents
   > Expected recovery = 0.5 × 80 + 0.5 × 20 = 50 cents.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A distressed bond trades at 40 and has an expected recovery of 50 cents. What is the expected return?
   - 10%
   - 50%
   - 20%
   - [x] 25%
   > Expected return = (50 − 40) / 40 = 25%.
