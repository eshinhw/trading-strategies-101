# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A 5-year annual-pay bond with a 5% coupon and $1,000 face value has a yield to maturity of 6%. What is its price?
   - [x] $957.88
   - $1,000
   - $918.00
   - $880.22
   > Discounting each coupon and the principal at 6% gives $957.88, below par because the coupon is below the yield.
