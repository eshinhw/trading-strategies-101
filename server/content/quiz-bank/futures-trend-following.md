# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trend follower with a $500,000 account risks 1% per trade. A market's average true range is 2.5 points, the stop is placed 2 ATR from entry, and each point is worth $50 per contract. How many contracts should be bought?
   - 40
   - 10
   - 200
   - [x] 20
   > Risk budget = 1% × $500,000 = $5,000. Risk per contract = 2 × 2.5 × $50 = $250. Contracts = $5,000 / $250 = 20.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trend follower buys at 100 and uses a trailing stop 10% below the highest price since entry. The price peaks at 118 and then falls. At what price is the position stopped out, and what is the profit per unit?
   - 108, a profit of 8
   - [x] 106.2, a profit of 6.2
   - 118, a profit of 18
   - 90, a loss of 10
   > The stop is 10% below the peak: 118 × 0.90 = 106.2. Profit = 106.2 − 100 = 6.2 per unit.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A trend-following system wins 40% of its trades with an average win of 3R and loses 60% of its trades with an average loss of 1R, where R is the amount risked per trade. What is the expectancy per trade?
   - +1.2R
   - −0.6R
   - +1.8R
   - [x] +0.6R
   > Expectancy = 0.40 × 3R − 0.60 × 1R = 1.2R − 0.6R = +0.6R per trade.
