# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A commodity trades at $80. The interest rate is 4%, storage costs are 2% and the convenience yield is 1% (all annual, continuous). What is the fair 6-month futures price?
   - [x] $82.03
   - $81.60
   - $80.00
   - $84.12
   > F = S × e^((r + storage − convenience yield) × T) = $80 × e^(0.05 × 0.5) = $82.03.

2. {#bk2} [calc] The fair 6-month futures price is $82.03 but the market price is $83.50. Is the future rich or cheap, and what does the model say to do?
   - Cheap by about $1.48: buy the future
   - Rich by about $3.50: sell the commodity
   - [x] Rich by about $1.48: sell the future and buy the commodity
   - Fair, so do nothing
   > The market price of $83.50 is 1.47 above the model value of $82.03, so the future is rich. The model suggests selling the future and holding the commodity.
