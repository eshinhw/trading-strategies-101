# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A loan shark lends $1,000 at 5% per week, compounded weekly. How much is owed after 4 weeks?
   - [x] $1,215.51
   - $1,200.00
   - $1,050.00
   - $1,250.00
   > $1,000 × 1.05⁴ = $1,215.51.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A loan shark charges 10% a week simple interest on a $2,000 loan. What is the interest after 6 weeks, and what is that as an annualized rate (52 weeks)?
   - $1,200, or 52% a year
   - $12,000, or 520% a year
   - [x] $1,200, or 520% a year
   - $200, or 10% a year
   > Interest = $2,000 × 10% × 6 = $1,200. Annualized simple rate = 10% × 52 = 520%.
