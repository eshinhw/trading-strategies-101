# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a Modified Put Butterfly: buys one $85 put, sells two $100 puts, and buys one $105 put, for a net credit of $1 per share. What is the maximum loss at expiration, per share?
   - $6
   - [x] $9
   - $1
   - $20
   > For this position (K1 = 85, K2 = 100, K3 = 105, net credit = 1), the maximum loss is $9 per share. Formula: L_max = 2K2 - K1 - K3 + H.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader builds a Modified Put Butterfly: buys one $85 put, sells two $100 puts, and buys one $105 put, for a net credit of $1 per share. What is the break-even stock price at expiration, per share?
   - $20
   - $9
   - $6
   - [x] $94
   > For this position (K1 = 85, K2 = 100, K3 = 105, net credit = 1), the break-even stock price is $94 per share. Formula: S*_down = 2K2 - K3 + H.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A trader builds a Modified Put Butterfly: buys one $85 put, sells two $100 puts, and buys one $105 put, for a net credit of $1 per share. At expiration the stock is at $113. What is the trader's profit or loss per share, counting the premium?

   - [x] +$1
   - +$3
   - +$2
