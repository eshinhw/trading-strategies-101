# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] EUR/USD is 1.0850 and GBP/USD is 1.2600. What is the EUR/GBP cross rate?
   - 1.1613
   - 1.3671
   - 0.8250
   - [x] 0.8611
   > EUR/GBP = EUR/USD ÷ GBP/USD = 1.0850 / 1.2600 = 0.8611.

2. {#bk2} [calc] EUR/USD is 1.1000 and GBP/USD is 1.2500. What is the EUR/GBP cross rate?
   - 1.1364
   - [x] 0.8800
   - 1.3750
   - 0.8500
   > EUR/GBP = EUR/USD ÷ GBP/USD = 1.1000 / 1.2500 = 0.8800.

3. {#bk3} [calc] EUR/USD is quoted 1.0848 bid and 1.0852 ask. What is the cost of buying and then immediately selling €1 million?
   - $4,000
   - $40
   - $200
   - [x] $400
   > The spread is 0.0004 per euro. On €1 million that is 0.0004 × 1,000,000 = $400.

4. {#bk4} [calc] USD/JPY is 150.00 and the 6-month forward points are −2.40. What is the 6-month forward rate?
   - [x] 147.60
   - 152.40
   - 150.00
   - 148.80
   > Forward rate = spot + forward points = 150.00 − 2.40 = 147.60 (yen trades at a forward premium because its interest rate is lower).

5. {#bk5} [calc] An importer must pay €2 million in 6 months. The 6-month forward rate is 1.1200. How many dollars does the importer lock in?
   - $1,785,714
   - $2,000,000
   - [x] $2,240,000
   - $2,200,000
   > Dollars needed = €2,000,000 × 1.1200 = $2,240,000.
