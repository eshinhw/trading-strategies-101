# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a Long Synthetic Forward: buys one $100 call and sells one $100 put, for no net premium. What is the maximum loss at expiration, per share?
   - $0
   - [x] $100
   - $101
   - $99
   > For this position (K = 100), the maximum loss is $100 per share. Formula: L_max = K - netCF.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader builds a Long Synthetic Forward: buys one $100 call and sells one $100 put, for no net premium. What is the maximum loss on 4 contracts (100 shares each), in dollars?
   - $400
   - $10,000
   - $50,000
   - [x] $40,000
   > The maximum loss is $100 per share. One contract covers 100 shares, so 4 contracts give $100 × 100 × 4 = $40,000.
