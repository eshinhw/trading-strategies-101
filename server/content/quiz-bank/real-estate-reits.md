# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A REIT has taxable income of $10 million and must distribute at least 90% of it. What is the minimum distribution?
   - $1 million
   - $10 million
   - $0.9 million
   - [x] $9 million
   > 90% × $10 million = $9 million must be paid to shareholders.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A REIT reports net income of $20 million, depreciation of $8 million, and gains on property sales of $3 million. What are its funds from operations (FFO)?
   - $31 million
   - [x] $25 million
   - $28 million
   - $17 million
   > FFO = net income + depreciation − gains on sales = $20 million + $8 million − $3 million = $25 million.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A REIT trades at $40 per share and pays $2.20 in annual dividends. What is the dividend yield?
   - 1.8%
   - 2.2%
   - 18.2%
   - [x] 5.5%
   > Dividend yield = $2.20 / $40 = 5.5%.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A REIT trades at $30 per share and generates funds from operations of $2.40 per share. What is its price-to-FFO multiple?
   - [x] 12.5
   - 0.08
   - 7.2
   - 72
   > $30 / $2.40 = 12.5.
