# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a Ratio Call Spread: sells two $100 calls and buys one $95 call, for a net credit of $2 per share. What is the maximum profit at expiration, per share?
   - $5
   - $2
   - [x] $7
   - $107
   > For this position (K2 = 95, K1 = 100, NL = 1, NS = 2, net credit = 2), the maximum profit is $7 per share. Formula: P_max = NL×(K1 - K2) + netCF.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader builds a Ratio Call Spread: sells two $100 calls and buys one $95 call, for a net credit of $2 per share. At expiration the stock is at $101. What is the trader's profit or loss per share, counting the premium?
   - [x] +$6
   - −$6
   - +$4
   - +$10
   > At $101 the option legs are worth +$4 per share before the premium, and the net premium adds +$2, for +$6 per share (given K2 = 95, K1 = 100, NL = 1, NS = 2, net credit = 2).

4. {#bk4} [calc] A trader builds a Ratio Call Spread: sells two $100 calls and buys one $95 call, for a net credit of $2 per share. What is the maximum profit on 10 contracts (100 shares each), in dollars?
