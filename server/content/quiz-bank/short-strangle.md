# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a Short Strangle: sells one $105 call and sells one $95 put, for a net credit of $4 per share. What is the maximum profit at expiration, per share?
   - $10
   - [x] $4
   - $91
   - $109
   > For this position (K1 = 105, K2 = 95, net credit = 4), the maximum profit is $4 per share. Formula: P_max = C.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader builds a Short Strangle: sells one $105 call and sells one $95 put, for a net credit of $4 per share. What is the upper break-even stock price at expiration, per share?
   - $91
   - $10
   - $4
   - [x] $109
   > For this position (K1 = 105, K2 = 95, net credit = 4), the upper break-even stock price is $109 per share. Formula: S*_up = K1 + C; S*_down = K2 - C.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A trader builds a Short Strangle: sells one $105 call and sells one $95 put, for a net credit of $4 per share. At expiration the stock is at $103. What is the trader's profit or loss per share, counting the premium?
   - −$4
   - [x] +$4
   - +$12
   - +$5
   > At $103 the option legs are worth $0 per share before the premium, and the net premium adds +$4, for +$4 per share (given K1 = 105, K2 = 95, net credit = 4).

5. {#bk5} [calc] A trader builds a Short Strangle: sells one $105 call and sells one $95 put, for a net credit of $4 per share. What is the maximum profit on 4 contracts (100 shares each), in dollars?
