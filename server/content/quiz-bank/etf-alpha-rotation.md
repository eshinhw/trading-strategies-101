# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] Two ETFs are scored 70% on momentum and 30% on value. ETF A has a momentum score of 80 and a value score of 40. ETF B has 60 and 70. Which has the higher combined score?
   - ETF B, 68 against 63
   - ETF A, 60 against 65
   - They tie at 64
   - [x] ETF A, 68 against 63
   > A = 0.7 × 80 + 0.3 × 40 = 68. B = 0.7 × 60 + 0.3 × 70 = 63. ETF A scores higher.

2. {#bk2} [calc] An alpha score blends momentum (weight 50%), value (30%) and low volatility (20%). An ETF scores 90, 50 and 30 on them. What is its combined score?
   - 56.7
   - [x] 66
   - 70
   - 50
   > Score = 0.5 × 90 + 0.3 × 50 + 0.2 × 30 = 45 + 15 + 6 = 66.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A rotation buys the 3 highest-scoring of 10 ETFs with equal weights. How much of the portfolio does each ETF get, and how much is not invested?
   - 10% each and 70% uninvested
   - 30% each and 10% uninvested
   - 3.3% each and 90% uninvested
   - [x] 33.3% each and 0% uninvested
   > Three ETFs equally weighted get 1/3 each, so the portfolio is fully invested.
