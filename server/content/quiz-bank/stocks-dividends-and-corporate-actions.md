# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A stock trades at $50 and pays $1.60 in dividends a year. What is its dividend yield?
   - [x] 3.20%
   - 6.40%
   - 1.60%
   - 1.65%
   > Dividend yield = annual dividend / price = $1.60 / $50 = 3.20%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A stock trades at $80 and pays $2.80 in dividends a year. What is its dividend yield?
   - 7.00%
   - 1.75%
   - [x] 3.50%
   - 1.81%
   > Dividend yield = annual dividend / price = $2.80 / $80 = 3.50%.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A stock trades at $125 and pays $3.75 in dividends a year. What is its dividend yield?
   - [x] 3.00%
   - 6.00%
   - 1.50%
   - 1.55%
   > Dividend yield = annual dividend / price = $3.75 / $125 = 3.00%.

4. {#bk4} [calc] An investor owns 500 shares at $120 before a 3-for-1 stock split. After the split, how many shares are held and at what approximate price?
   - 166 shares at $360
   - 1,500 shares at $120
   - 500 shares at $40
   - [x] 1,500 shares at $40
   > A 3-for-1 split multiplies shares by 3 and divides the price by 3: 500 × 3 = 1,500 shares, $120 / 3 = $40. The total value is unchanged at $60,000.

5. {#bk5} [calc] An investor owns 1,000 shares at $90 before a 2-for-1 stock split. After the split, how many shares are held and at what approximate price?
   - 500 shares at $180
   - [x] 2,000 shares at $45
   - 2,000 shares at $90
   - 1,000 shares at $45
   > A 2-for-1 split multiplies shares by 2 and divides the price by 2: 1,000 × 2 = 2,000 shares, $90 / 2 = $45. The total value is unchanged at $90,000.

6. {#bk6} [calc] A stock closes at $60.00 the day before it goes ex-dividend with a $1.20 dividend. Ignoring other price moves, what is a reasonable opening price on the ex-dividend date?
   - $61.20
   - $60.00
   - $59.40
   - [x] $58.80
   > On the ex-dividend date the share price drops by about the dividend: $60.00 − $1.20 = $58.80.
