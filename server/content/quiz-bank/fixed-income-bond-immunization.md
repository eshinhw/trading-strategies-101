# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A liability has a duration of 5 years. An investor funds it with zero-coupon bonds of duration 2 and 10 years. What fraction of assets goes in the 2-year bond to match duration?
   - 37.5%
   - 50%
   - [x] 62.5%
   - 25%
   > Solve w × 2 + (1 − w) × 10 = 5, so w = (10 − 5) / (10 − 2) = 62.5%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A liability has a duration of 5 years. An investor funds it with zero-coupon bonds of duration 3 and 9 years. What fraction of assets goes in the 3-year bond to match duration?
   - [x] 66.7%
   - 33.3%
   - 50%
   - 55.6%
   > Solve w × 3 + (1 − w) × 9 = 5, so w = (9 − 5) / (9 − 3) = 66.7%.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A liability has a duration of 2 years. An investor funds it with zero-coupon bonds of duration 1 and 7 years. What fraction of assets goes in the 1-year bond to match duration?
   - 16.7%
   - 50%
   - [x] 83.3%
   - 28.6%
   > Solve w × 1 + (1 − w) × 7 = 2, so w = (7 − 2) / (7 − 1) = 83.3%.
