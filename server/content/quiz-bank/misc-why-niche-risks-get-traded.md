# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A catastrophe bond pays a 7% coupon on $1,000 and loses all principal with a 2% annual probability of a qualifying event. What is the expected annual return (ignoring recovery)?
   - 7%
   - [x] 4.9%
   - −2%
   - 9%
   > Expected return = 98% × 7% + 2% × (−100%) = 6.86% − 2% = 4.86%, about 4.9%.
