# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader is short $30,000 of a VIX ETN. The ETN moves 0.7 times as much as VIX futures in percentage terms. What dollar amount of VIX futures hedges the position?
   - [x] $21,000
   - $42,857
   - $30,000
   - $9,000
   > Hedge notional = 0.7 × $30,000 = $21,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader is short $30,000 of a VIX ETN hedged with $21,000 of long VIX futures. Volatility spikes: the ETN rises 40% and the futures rise 50%. What is the net result?
   - −$12,000
   - +$10,500
   - [x] −$1,500
   - −$22,500
   > The short ETN loses 40% × $30,000 = $12,000. The futures gain 50% × $21,000 = $10,500. Net = −$1,500.
