# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A straight bond from the issuer yields 6% while its convertible pays a 3% coupon on $1,000. How much yearly income does the investor give up for the conversion option?
   - $60
   - $90
   - $3
   - [x] $30
   > The straight bond would pay 6% × $1,000 = $60 and the convertible pays $30, so the investor gives up $30 a year.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A convertible bond pays a 2.5% coupon on $1,000 face value in two semiannual payments. How much does each payment equal?
   - $25.00
   - [x] $12.50
   - $2.50
   - $1.25
   > Annual coupon is $25, paid in two parts: $25 / 2 = $12.50.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A convertible bond's conversion ratio is 20. The stock trades at $45, and the bond's parity price is calculated by conversion. What is the parity value?
   - $225
   - $450
   - $1,000
   - [x] $900
   > Parity = ratio × stock price = 20 × $45 = $900.
