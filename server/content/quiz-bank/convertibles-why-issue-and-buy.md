# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A company issues $100 million of convertibles at 2% instead of straight debt at 6%. How much does it save in interest each year?
   - $2 million
   - $6 million
   - $8 million
   - [x] $4 million
   > Straight debt costs 6% × $100 million = $6 million, and the convertible costs $2 million, a $4 million saving.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A company issues $50 million of convertibles with a conversion price of $25. If all convert, how many new shares are created?
   - 1.25 million
   - [x] 2 million
   - 50 million
   - 25 million
   > New shares = $50 million / $25 = 2 million.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A convertible has a conversion ratio of 20. The investor paid $1,000 and the stock rises 30% from $45 to $58.50. What is the conversion value?
   - $1,300
   - $900
   - $1,000
   - [x] $1,170
   > Conversion value = 20 × $58.50 = $1,170, a gain of $170 on the $1,000 paid.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A company issues $200 million of 1.5% convertibles instead of 5.5% straight debt. How much interest does it save in the first year?
   - [x] $8 million
   - $3 million
   - $11 million
   - $4 million
   > The rate difference is 4%, and 4% × $200 million = $8 million.
