# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] EUR/USD is at 1.0850 and its 100-day moving average is 1.0780. How far above the average is the price?
   - 0.7 cents
   - [x] 0.65%
   - 6.5%
   - 0.065%
   > (1.0850 − 1.0780) / 1.0780 = 0.65%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A moving-average rule goes long EUR/USD when the price is above the average. A trader goes long €1,000,000 at 1.0850 and exits at 1.0900. What is the profit?
   - $500
   - $50,000
   - $1,090,000
   - [x] $5,000
   > Profit = (1.0900 − 1.0850) × €1,000,000 = $5,000.

3. {#bk3} [calc] The last five daily closes of EUR/USD are 1.0800, 1.0820, 1.0850, 1.0830 and 1.0860. What is the 5-day moving average?
   - 1.0850
   - [x] 1.0832
   - 1.0860
   - 1.0800
   > (1.0800 + 1.0820 + 1.0850 + 1.0830 + 1.0860) / 5 = 5.4160 / 5 = 1.0832.
