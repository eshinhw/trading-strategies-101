# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader earns $1,000,000 carry on a long equity tranche and pays 100 basis points a year on $30 million of index protection. What is the net carry?
   - [x] $700,000
   - $300,000
   - $1,300,000
   - $970,000
   > Protection costs 1% × $30 million = $300,000. Net carry = $1,000,000 − $300,000 = $700,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader earns 25% a year on a $4 million equity tranche and buys $20 million of index protection at 60 basis points. What is the net carry?
   - $1,000,000
   - $120,000
   - [x] $880,000
   - $1,120,000
   > Tranche income = 25% × $4 million = $1,000,000. Hedge cost = 0.6% × $20 million = $120,000. Net = $880,000.
