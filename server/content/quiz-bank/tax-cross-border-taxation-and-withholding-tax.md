# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A foreign company pays an investor a $10,000 dividend with 30% withholding tax. What does the investor receive? What if a treaty lowers the rate to 15%?
   - $7,000, or $9,000 under the treaty
   - $3,000, or $1,500 under the treaty
   - [x] $7,000, or $8,500 under the treaty
   - $7,000, or $7,500 under the treaty
   > At 30% withholding the investor receives $10,000 × 0.70 = $7,000. At 15% they receive $10,000 × 0.85 = $8,500.

2. {#bk2} [calc] An investor earns $10,000 of foreign dividends taxed at 15% abroad ($1,500) and at 24% at home. With a foreign tax credit, how much additional home tax is owed?
   - [x] $900
   - $2,400
   - $1,500
   - $3,900
   > Home tax is 24% × $10,000 = $2,400. The foreign tax credit removes $1,500, leaving $900 owed.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A foreign stock yields a 4% dividend and 15% is withheld at source. What is the net dividend yield?
   - 0.6%
   - 4.6%
   - [x] 3.4%
   - 3.85%
   > Net yield = 4% × (1 − 0.15) = 3.4%.
