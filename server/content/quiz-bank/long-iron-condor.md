4. {#bk4} [calc] A trader builds a Long Iron Condor: buys one $85 put, sells one $95 put, sells one $105 call, and buys one $115 call, for a net credit of $4 per share. At expiration the stock is at $77. What is the trader's profit or loss per share, counting the premium?
   - +$6
   - −$10
   - +$2
   - [x] −$6
   > At $77 the option legs are worth −$10 per share before the premium, and the net premium adds +$4, for −$6 per share (given K1 = 85, K2 = 95, K3 = 105, K4 = 115, net credit = 4).

6. {#bk6} [calc] A trader builds a Long Iron Condor: buys one $85 put, sells one $95 put, sells one $105 call, and buys one $115 call, for a net credit of $4 per share. What is the maximum profit on 3 contracts (100 shares each), in dollars?
