# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] Five monthly returns for a commodity are −6%, −1%, 0%, +1% and +2%. Skewness is the average cubed deviation divided by the variance to the power of 1.5. Is the skewness positive or negative, and about how large?
   - Positive, about 1.04
   - Zero
   - Negative, about −3.1
   - [x] Negative, about −1.04
   > The mean is −0.8. The deviations are −5.2, −0.2, 0.8, 1.8 and 2.8. The cubed deviations average −22.46 and the variance is 7.76, so skewness = −22.46 / 21.62 = −1.04, a negative skew from the one large loss.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A skewness strategy buys commodities with the most negative skew and sells those with the most positive skew, on the idea that investors overpay for positive skew. Commodity A has skew −1.2 and B has skew +0.8. What does the rule do?
   - Buys B and sells A
   - [x] Buys A and sells B
   - Buys both
   - Sells both
   > A has the most negative skew, so the rule buys it. B has the most positive skew, so the rule sells it.
