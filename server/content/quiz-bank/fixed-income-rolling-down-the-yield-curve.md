# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A 5-year bond yields 4.00% and the curve is upward sloping: after one year it will be a bond yielding 3.70% on an unchanged curve, with duration about 4.5. Roughly what is the one-year return from carry plus roll-down?
   - 4.00%
   - [x] 5.35%
   - 1.35%
   - 6.70%
   > Roll-down gain ≈ 4.5 × (4.00% − 3.70%) = 1.35%. Adding the 4.00% yield gives 5.35%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A 4-year bond yields 3.50% and the curve is upward sloping: after one year it will be a bond yielding 3.00% on an unchanged curve, with duration about 3.8. Roughly what is the one-year return from carry plus roll-down?
   - 3.50%
   - 1.90%
   - 7.30%
   - [x] 5.40%
   > Roll-down gain ≈ 3.8 × (3.50% − 3.00%) = 1.90%. Adding the 3.50% yield gives 5.40%.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A 6-year bond yields 4.50% and the curve is upward sloping: after one year it will be a bond yielding 4.10% on an unchanged curve, with duration about 5.2. Roughly what is the one-year return from carry plus roll-down?
   - 4.50%
   - [x] 6.58%
   - 2.08%
   - 8.66%
   > Roll-down gain ≈ 5.2 × (4.50% − 4.10%) = 2.08%. Adding the 4.50% yield gives 6.58%.
