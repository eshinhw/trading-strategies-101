# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a Long Straddle: buys one $100 call and buys one $100 put, for a net debit of $6 per share. What is the lower break-even stock price at expiration, per share?
   - [x] $94
   - $106
   - $6
   - $0
   > For this position (K = 100, net debit = 6), the lower break-even stock price is $94 per share. Formula: S*_up = K + D; S*_down = K - D.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A trader builds a Long Straddle: buys one $100 call and buys one $100 put, for a net debit of $6 per share. At expiration the stock is at $103. What is the trader's profit or loss per share, counting the premium?
   - [x] −$3
   - +$3
   - +$9
   - −$2
   > At $103 the option legs are worth +$3 per share before the premium, and the net premium adds −$6, for −$3 per share (given K = 100, net debit = 6).

5. {#bk5} [calc] A trader builds a Long Straddle: buys one $100 call and buys one $100 put, for a net debit of $6 per share. What is the maximum loss on 4 contracts (100 shares each), in dollars?
