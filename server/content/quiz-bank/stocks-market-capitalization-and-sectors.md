# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A company has 2.5 billion shares outstanding at $84 each. What is its market capitalization?
   - $2,100 billion
   - [x] $210 billion
   - $21 billion
   - $420 billion
   > Market cap = shares outstanding × share price = 2,500,000,000 × $84 = $210 billion.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A company has 480 million shares outstanding at $35 each. What is its market capitalization?
   - $168 billion
   - $1.7 billion
   - $33.6 billion
   - [x] $16.8 billion
   > Market cap = shares outstanding × share price = 480,000,000 × $35 = $16.8 billion.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A company has 60 million shares outstanding at $22.50 each. What is its market capitalization?
   - $13.5 billion
   - [x] $1.35 billion
   - $135 million
   - $2.7 billion
   > Market cap = shares outstanding × share price = 60,000,000 × $22.50 = $1.35 billion.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A market-cap-weighted fund holds Stock A ($300 billion market cap) and Stock B ($100 billion market cap) and nothing else. What is Stock B's weight?
   - 50%
   - 33%
   - [x] 25%
   - 10%
   > Weights follow market cap: $100 billion / ($300 billion + $100 billion) = 25%.
