# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader owns 10 call option contracts (100 shares each) with a vega of $0.12 per volatility point per share. If implied volatility rises 3 points, what is the gain?
   - $36
   - $3,600
   - $120
   - [x] $360
   > Gain = 3 points × $0.12 × 100 shares × 10 contracts = $360.
