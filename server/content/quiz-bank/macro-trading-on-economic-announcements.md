# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] Payrolls come in at 250,000 jobs against a consensus forecast of 180,000. What is the surprise?
   - −70,000 jobs
   - +250,000 jobs
   - +38.9%
   - [x] +70,000 jobs
   > The surprise is actual minus expected: 250,000 − 180,000 = +70,000 jobs.

2. {#bk2} [calc] Core inflation is released at 3.4% against an expected 3.2%. If every 0.1% surprise historically moves 2-year yields by 3 basis points, what is the expected yield move?
   - +3 basis points
   - [x] +6 basis points
   - +0.6 basis points
   - +60 basis points
   > The surprise is 0.2%, or two 0.1% units. 2 × 3 basis points = +6 basis points.
