# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A regional price index rises from 110 to 118 over 12 months. What is its 12-month momentum?
   - 8%
   - 6.78%
   - 0.73%
   - [x] 7.27%
   > (118 − 110) / 110 = 7.27%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A regional momentum strategy holds the 2 best of 4 regions equally weighted. The 12-month price growth rates are North +7%, South +4%, East +2% and West −1%. Next year North earns +5% and South earns +3%. What is the portfolio return?
   - 3.5%
   - [x] 4%
   - 5%
   - 2.5%
   > The best two are North and South. Equal weights give (5% + 3%) / 2 = 4%.

3. {#bk3} [calc] Regional A rose 9% last year and regional B rose 3%. A strategy overweights the momentum winner: 70% in A and 30% in B. If both repeat last year's growth, what is the portfolio growth?
   - 6%
   - 9%
   - 5.4%
   - [x] 7.2%
   > 0.70 × 9% + 0.30 × 3% = 6.3% + 0.9% = 7.2%.
