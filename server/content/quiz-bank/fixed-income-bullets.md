# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A bullet portfolio puts $10 million in 5-year bonds with a modified duration of 4.5. Yields rise 0.5%. What is the approximate loss?
   - $22,500
   - $2,250,000
   - [x] $225,000
   - $45,000
   > Loss ≈ duration × yield change × value = 4.5 × 0.005 × $10 million = $225,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A $10 million bullet portfolio of 5-year bonds yields 4.0%. What is the approximate yearly coupon income?
   - [x] $400,000
   - $40,000
   - $4,000,000
   - $200,000
   > Income ≈ 4.0% × $10 million = $400,000.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A bullet in 5-year bonds has a duration of 4.5. A barbell uses 2-year bonds (duration 1.9) and 10-year bonds (duration 8.0). What share in the 2-year bond gives the barbell the same duration as the bullet?
   - 50%
   - 42.6%
   - [x] 57.4%
   - 65%
   > Solve w × 1.9 + (1 − w) × 8.0 = 4.5, so w = (8.0 − 4.5) / (8.0 − 1.9) = 3.5 / 6.1 = 57.4%.
