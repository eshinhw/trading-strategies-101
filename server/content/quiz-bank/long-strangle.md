# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a Long Strangle: buys one $105 call and buys one $95 put, for a net debit of $4 per share. What is the maximum loss at expiration, per share?
   - [x] $4
   - $10
   - $91
   - $109
   > For this position (K1 = 105, K2 = 95, net debit = 4), the maximum loss is $4 per share. Formula: L_max = D.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader builds a Long Strangle: buys one $105 call and buys one $95 put, for a net debit of $4 per share. What is the upper break-even stock price at expiration, per share?
   - $91
   - $10
   - [x] $109
   - $4
   > For this position (K1 = 105, K2 = 95, net debit = 4), the upper break-even stock price is $109 per share. Formula: S*_up = K1 + D; S*_down = K2 - D.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A trader builds a Long Strangle: buys one $105 call and buys one $95 put, for a net debit of $4 per share. At expiration the stock is at $113. What is the trader's profit or loss per share, counting the premium?
   - [x] +$4
   - −$4
   - +$8
   - +$12
   > At $113 the option legs are worth +$8 per share before the premium, and the net premium adds −$4, for +$4 per share (given K1 = 105, K2 = 95, net debit = 4).

4. {#bk4} [calc] A trader builds a Long Strangle: buys one $105 call and buys one $95 put, for a net debit of $4 per share. What is the maximum loss on 5 contracts (100 shares each), in dollars?
   - $20
   - $400
   - $2,400
   - [x] $2,000
   > The maximum loss is $4 per share. One contract covers 100 shares, so 5 contracts give $4 × 100 × 5 = $2,000.
