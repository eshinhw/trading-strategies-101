# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] VIX spot is 17.50 and the front VIX future is 19.00. What is the futures basis relative to spot?
   - −1.50 points
   - [x] 1.50 points
   - 0.50 points
   - 3.00 points
   > The basis is futures minus spot: 19.00 − 17.50 = 1.50 points (contango).
