# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a Short Guts: sells one $95 call and sells one $105 put, for a net credit of $14 per share. What is the maximum profit at expiration, per share?
   - $10
   - $14
   - $91
   - [x] $4
   > For this position (K1 = 95, K2 = 105, net credit = 14), the maximum profit is $4 per share. Formula: P_max = C - (K2 - K1).

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader builds a Short Guts: sells one $95 call and sells one $105 put, for a net credit of $14 per share. What is the upper break-even stock price at expiration, per share?
   - $91
   - [x] $109
   - $14
   - $10
   > For this position (K1 = 95, K2 = 105, net credit = 14), the upper break-even stock price is $109 per share. Formula: S*_up = K1 + C; S*_down = K2 - C.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A trader builds a Short Guts: sells one $95 call and sells one $105 put, for a net credit of $14 per share. At expiration the stock is at $103. What is the trader's profit or loss per share, counting the premium?
   - −$4
   - −$10
   - +$32
   - [x] +$4
   > At $103 the option legs are worth −$10 per share before the premium, and the net premium adds +$14, for +$4 per share (given K1 = 95, K2 = 105, net credit = 14).
