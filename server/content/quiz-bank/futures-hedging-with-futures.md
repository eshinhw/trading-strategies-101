# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A corn farmer expects 120,000 bushels. How many corn futures of 5,000 bushels fully hedge the exposure?
   - 240
   - 2
   - [x] 24
   - 29
   > The number of contracts is the exposure divided by the contract size: 120,000 / 5,000 = 24.
