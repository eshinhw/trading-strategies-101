# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] Income taxed at 40% in Country A could instead be taxed at 20% in Country B. How much tax is saved by shifting $1 million of income?
   - [x] $200,000
   - $400,000
   - $600,000
   - $20,000
   > The saving is the rate difference times the income: (40% − 20%) × $1,000,000 = $200,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A company can deduct $2 million of interest at a 25% tax rate. What is the tax saving?
   - $2,000,000
   - $1,500,000
   - [x] $500,000
   - $250,000
   > Tax saving = $2,000,000 × 25% = $500,000.
