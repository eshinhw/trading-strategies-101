# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A convertible arbitrage trader owns 100 convertible bonds, each convertible into 20 shares, with an option delta of 0.6. How many shares should be shorted to be delta-neutral?
   - [x] 1,200 shares
   - 2,000 shares
   - 60 shares
   - 800 shares
   > Shares to short = bonds × ratio × delta = 100 × 20 × 0.6 = 1,200.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A convertible arbitrage trader owns 50 convertible bonds, each convertible into 16 shares, with an option delta of 0.4. How many shares should be shorted to be delta-neutral?
   - 800 shares
   - 20 shares
   - [x] 320 shares
   - 480 shares
   > Shares to short = bonds × ratio × delta = 50 × 16 × 0.4 = 320.
