# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] Power sells for $50/MWh and natural gas costs $3.5/MMBtu. A gas plant has a heat rate of 7 MMBtu per MWh. What is the spark spread?
   - $46.5/MWh
   - [x] $25.5/MWh
   - $24.5/MWh
   - $51/MWh
   > Spark spread = power price − gas price × heat rate = 50 − 3.5 × 7 = 25.5 dollars per MWh.

2. {#bk2} [calc] Power sells for $42/MWh and natural gas costs $4.5/MMBtu. A gas plant has a heat rate of 7 MMBtu per MWh. What is the spark spread?
   - $37.5/MWh
   - $31.5/MWh
   - $21/MWh
   - [x] $10.5/MWh
   > Spark spread = power price − gas price × heat rate = 42 − 4.5 × 7 = 10.5 dollars per MWh.

   - $60/MWh
   - [x] $25/MWh
   - $40/MWh
   - $50/MWh
   > Spark spread = power price − gas price × heat rate = 65 − 5.0 × 8 = 25 dollars per MWh.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A power plant with a spark spread of $14/MWh runs 400 MWh a day. What is the daily gross margin?
   - $560
   - $414
   - [x] $5,600
   - $28
   > Margin = $14 × 400 = $5,600 before fixed costs.

5. {#bk5} [calc] A gas plant's heat rate is 7.5 MMBtu per MWh. Gas costs $3.20 per MMBtu and power sells for $41. What is the spark spread?
   - [x] $17/MWh
   - $37.80/MWh
   - $24/MWh
   - $41/MWh
   > Spark spread = $41 − 7.5 × $3.20 = $41 − $24 = $17 per MWh.
