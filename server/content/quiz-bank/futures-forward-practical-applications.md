# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An airline buys 2 million gallons of fuel forward at $2.90 per gallon. At delivery spot is $3.30. What is the value of the hedge?
   - $80,000
   - [x] $800,000
   - $1,600,000
   - $8,000,000
   > The airline pays $0.40 less per gallon: $0.40 × 2,000,000 = $800,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A company will receive €5 million in 3 months and sells euros forward at 1.0900. At delivery spot is 1.0600. How much more does the hedge deliver than selling at spot?
   - $15,000
   - $1,500,000
   - $300,000
   - [x] $150,000
   > (1.0900 − 1.0600) × €5,000,000 = $150,000.

3. {#bk3} [calc] An importer owes €2,000,000 and buys euros forward at 1.0950. At maturity spot is 1.0700. How much more does the hedge cost than buying at spot?
   - $5,000
   - [x] $50,000
   - $500,000
   - $2,190,000
   > The forward costs 1.0950 − 1.0700 = $0.0250 more per euro: $0.0250 × 2,000,000 = $50,000.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A company locks in a 6-month borrowing rate of 4.50% on $10 million with an FRA. The market rate turns out to be 5.20%. How much interest does the FRA save over the 6 months (before discounting)?
   - $70,000
   - $350,000
   - [x] $35,000
   - $3,500
   > The rate saved is 0.70%. $10,000,000 × 0.70% × 0.5 = $35,000.

5. {#bk5} [calc] A producer sells 100,000 gallons of heating oil forward at $2.80. At delivery spot is $2.55. What is the gain versus selling at spot?
   - [x] $25,000
   - $250,000
   - $2,500
   - $280,000
   > ($2.80 − $2.55) × 100,000 = $25,000.

6. {#bk6} [calc] A grower must hedge exactly 12,500 bushels. A standard contract is 5,000 bushels. If the grower uses 2 standard contracts, how many bushels stay unhedged?
   - 0
   - 5,000
   - [x] 2,500
   - 7,500
   > Two contracts hedge 10,000 bushels. 12,500 − 10,000 = 2,500 bushels stay unhedged. A custom forward could match 12,500 exactly.
