# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An investor buys a $60 million loan at 70 cents, then swaps it for 80% of the reorganized company's equity, which is valued at $75 million. What is the profit?
   - [x] $18,000,000
   - $33,000,000
   - $60,000,000
   - $48,000,000
   > Cost = $60 million × 0.70 = $42 million. Equity received = 80% × $75 million = $60 million. Profit = $60 million − $42 million = $18 million.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A lender buys a $40 million loan at 60 cents and later converts it into 100% ownership of a company worth $30 million. What is the gain or loss?
   - $6,000,000 loss
   - $16,000,000 gain
   - [x] $6,000,000 gain
   - $10,000,000 gain
   > Cost = $40 million × 0.60 = $24 million. Ownership is worth $30 million. Gain = $6 million.
