# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A bankrupt company has $100 million available to creditors. Secured creditors are owed $60 million and unsecured creditors $80 million. How many cents on the dollar do unsecured creditors recover?
   - 75 cents
   - 100 cents
   - [x] 50 cents
   - 57 cents
   > Secured creditors are paid first: $60 million. The remaining $40 million goes to unsecured creditors owed $80 million: 40/80 = 50 cents.

2. {#bk2} [calc] Senior debt of $50 million and junior debt of $50 million share a $70 million recovery. What percentage of the junior debt is repaid?
   - [x] 40%
   - 70%
   - 100%
   - 20%
   > Senior debt takes its $50 million first. The remaining $20 million goes to junior debt: $20 million / $50 million = 40%.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A company is worth $60 million in bankruptcy. Claims are $30 million secured, $40 million senior unsecured, and $30 million subordinated. What fraction of the senior unsecured claim is recovered?
   - 60%
   - 100%
   - [x] 75%
   - 50%
   > Secured claims take $30 million. The remaining $30 million goes to senior unsecured creditors: $30 million / $40 million = 75%. Subordinated creditors receive nothing.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A creditor holds a $10 million unsecured claim that is expected to recover 35 cents on the dollar. How much does the creditor expect to receive?
   - $6.5 million
   - [x] $3.5 million
   - $35 million
   - $0.35 million
   > Recovery = $10 million × 35% = $3.5 million.
