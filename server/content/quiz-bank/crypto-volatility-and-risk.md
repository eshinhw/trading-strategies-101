# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A cryptocurrency's daily returns have a standard deviation of 4%. Crypto trades every day, so annualize with 365 days. What is the annualized volatility?
   - 64.0%
   - 4%
   - 1,460%
   - [x] 76.4%
   > Annualized volatility = 4% × √365 = 4% × 19.1 = 76.4%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A coin falls from a peak of $60,000 to $30,000. What is the drawdown, and what gain is needed to get back to the peak?
   - −50% and a +50% gain
   - [x] −50% and a +100% gain
   - −100% and a +50% gain
   - −30% and a +30% gain
   > The drawdown is (30,000 − 60,000) / 60,000 = −50%. Getting back needs $30,000 → $60,000, a 100% gain.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A trader risks 1% of a $50,000 account on a coin and places a stop 10% below the entry. How large a position should be opened?
   - $500
   - $50,000
   - $25,000
   - [x] $5,000
   > Risk budget = 1% × $50,000 = $500. A 10% stop means the position is $500 / 0.10 = $5,000.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A $20,000 position has a daily volatility of 5%. Using 1.645 standard deviations for a 95% one-day value at risk, what is the VaR?
   - [x] $1,645
   - $1,000
   - $3,290
   - $16,450
   > VaR = 1.645 × 5% × $20,000 = $1,645.
