# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A taxable bond yields 4.2% and a municipal bond yields 3.0%. An investor is taxed at 30%. Which gives the higher after-tax yield, and what is it?
   - The taxable bond at 4.20%
   - [x] The municipal bond at 3.00%
   - The taxable bond at 2.94%
   - They are equal
   > After tax the taxable bond yields 4.2% × 0.70 = 2.94%. The municipal bond keeps its full 3.00%, which is higher.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader borrows $1 million at 5%, deducting interest at a 30% tax rate, and invests in tax-exempt municipal bonds yielding 4%. What is the annual after-tax profit?
   - $10,000
   - $15,000
   - −$10,000
   - [x] $5,000
   > After-tax borrowing cost is 5% × 0.70 = 3.5%, or $35,000. Muni income is 4% × $1 million = $40,000. Profit = $5,000.
