# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A buyer agrees to buy 1,000 ounces of gold forward at $1,980. At settlement gold trades at $2,020. What is the buyer's gain?
   - $4,000
   - $2,020,000
   - [x] $40,000
   - $1,980,000
   > The buyer pays $1,980 for gold worth $2,020: ($2,020 − $1,980) × 1,000 = $40,000.
