# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An investor buys distressed bonds at 40 cents on the dollar, and the bonds later recover 65 cents. What is the return?
   - [x] 62.5%
   - 25%
   - 162.5%
   - 38.5%
   > Return = (65 − 40) / 40 = 62.5%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A bond with an 8% coupon on a $100 face trades at 50. What is the current yield?
   - 8%
   - 4%
   - [x] 16%
   - 50%
   > Current yield = annual coupon / price = $8 / $50 = 16%, though the coupon may not be paid in distress.

3. {#bk3} [calc] An investor buys $5 million of face value at 45 and later receives 60 cents on the dollar. What is the profit?
   - [x] $750,000
   - $3,000,000
   - $2,250,000
   - $1,000,000
   > Cost = $5 million × 0.45 = $2.25 million. Proceeds = $5 million × 0.60 = $3.0 million. Profit = $750,000.
