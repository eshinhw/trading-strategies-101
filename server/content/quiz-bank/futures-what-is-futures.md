# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader buys 6 E-mini S&P 500 contracts (multiplier $50) at 4,200, and the price later moves to 4,236. What is the profit or loss on the position?
   - +$219
   - +$1,825
   - −$10,950
   - [x] +$10,950
   > The move is 4,236 − 4,200 = 36.50 per unit, and 36.50 × 50 × 6 contracts = $10,950.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader buys 3 corn contracts (5,000 bushels each) at 5.80, and the price later moves to 6.15. What is the profit or loss on the position?
   - +$1.05
   - [x] +$5,250
   - +$1,750
   - −$5,250
   > The move is 6.15 − 5.80 = 0.35 per unit, and 0.35 × 5,000 × 3 contracts = $5,250.
