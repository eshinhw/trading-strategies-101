# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader buys 10 call contracts (100 shares each) with a $50 strike for $3 each. The stock expires at $58. What is the total profit?
   - $8,000
   - $3,000
   - $500
   - [x] $5,000
   > Profit per share = $58 − $50 − $3 = $5. Total = $5 × 100 × 10 = $5,000.
