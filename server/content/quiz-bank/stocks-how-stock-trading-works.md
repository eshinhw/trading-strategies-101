# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A stock is quoted 24.98 bid and 25.02 ask. An investor buys 2,000 shares at the ask and sells them immediately at the bid. What is the round-trip loss?
   - $40
   - $160
   - $8
   - [x] $80
   > The loss is the spread on every share: (25.02 − 24.98) × 2,000 = $80.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A stock is quoted 101.10 bid and 101.30 ask. An investor buys 500 shares at the ask and sells them immediately at the bid. What is the round-trip loss?
   - $50
   - [x] $100
   - $200
   - $10
   > The loss is the spread on every share: (101.30 − 101.10) × 500 = $100.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A stock is quoted 9.40 bid and 9.46 ask. An investor buys 10,000 shares at the ask and sells them immediately at the bid. What is the round-trip loss?
   - $300
   - $1,200
   - $60
   - [x] $600
   > The loss is the spread on every share: (9.46 − 9.40) × 10,000 = $600.

4. {#bk4} [calc] An investor has $20,000 in a margin account and buys stock worth $40,000 using the maximum 2:1 buying power. If the stock then falls 10%, what is the investor's equity?
   - [x] $16,000
   - $18,000
   - $36,000
   - $4,000
   > The position drops 10% × $40,000 = $4,000. Equity is $20,000 − $4,000 = $16,000, a 20% loss on the investor's own money.

5. {#bk5} [calc] A limit order to buy 300 shares at $42.00 is placed while the stock trades at $42.40. The stock falls to $41.90 and the order fills. How much is paid in total at best?
   - $12,720
   - $12,570
   - [x] $12,600
   - $12,630
   > A buy limit order fills at the limit price or better, so at best the order pays $42.00 × 300 = $12,600 (it fills at or below $42.00).
