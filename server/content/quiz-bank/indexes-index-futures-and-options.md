# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An S&P 500 E-mini future (multiplier $50) trades at 4,500. What is the notional value of one contract?
   - [x] $225,000
   - $4,500
   - $22,500
   - $450,000
   > Notional = index level × multiplier = 4,500 × $50 = $225,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] An index is at 4,500, the interest rate is 5%, the dividend yield is 1.5%, and the future expires in 3 months. What is the fair futures price (continuous compounding)?
   - 4,500.0
   - 4,573.7
   - [x] 4,539.5
   - 4,556.2
   > Fair value = 4,500 × e^((5% − 1.5%) × 0.25) = 4,539.5.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A trader is long 10 E-mini S&P 500 contracts (multiplier $50). The index rises 30 points. What is the profit?
   - [x] $15,000
   - $1,500
   - $300
   - $150,000
   > Profit = 30 points × $50 × 10 contracts = $15,000.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A manager holds $9,000,000 of stocks with a beta of 1.2. The index is at 4,500 and the futures multiplier is $50. How many index futures contracts should be sold to fully hedge market risk?
   - 40
   - 57.6
   - 96
   - [x] 48
   > Contracts = beta × portfolio value / (index × multiplier) = 1.2 × $9,000,000 / (4,500 × 50) = 48.

5. {#bk5} [calc] A manager holds $20,000,000 of stocks with a beta of 1.5. The index is at 4,000 and the futures multiplier is $50. How many index futures contracts should be sold to fully hedge market risk?
   - 100
   - [x] 150
   - 225
   - 300
   > Contracts = beta × portfolio value / (index × multiplier) = 1.5 × $20,000,000 / (4,000 × 50) = 150.
