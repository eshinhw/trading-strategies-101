# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A dealer borrows $10,000,000 overnight-style for 7 days in a repo at 5% (actual/360). What is the interest owed?
   - $9,589
   - $41,667
   - [x] $9,722
   - $97,222
   > Interest = $10,000,000 × 5% × 7/360 = $9,722.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A dealer borrows $25,000,000 overnight-style for 14 days in a repo at 4.25% (actual/360). What is the interest owed?
   - [x] $41,319
   - $40,753
   - $88,542
   - $413,194
   > Interest = $25,000,000 × 4.25% × 14/360 = $41,319.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A dealer sells a bond to a lender at 100.00 per $100 face in a repo and agrees to buy it back at 100.05 three days later. What is the annualized repo rate (actual/360)?
   - 0.05%
   - 5.00%
   - [x] 6.00%
   - 0.60%
   > The 3-day return is 0.05 / 100 = 0.05%. Annualized: 0.05% × 360 / 3 = 6.00%.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A repo lender takes $10 million of bonds as collateral and applies a 2% haircut. How much cash does the borrower receive?
   - $10.2 million
   - [x] $9.8 million
   - $9.2 million
   - $10 million
   > The haircut means lending less than the collateral value: $10 million × (1 − 2%) = $9.8 million.
