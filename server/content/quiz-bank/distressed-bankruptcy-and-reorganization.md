# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A reorganized company is worth $120 million. Secured debt of $70 million is repaid in full, and the remaining value goes to $100 million of unsecured debt. What percentage of unsecured debt is recovered?
   - 120%
   - [x] 50%
   - 70%
   - 30%
   > After the secured debt's $70 million, $50 million remains for unsecured creditors: $50 million / $100 million = 50%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A bond with a face value of $1,000 recovers 30 cents on the dollar in bankruptcy. How much does a bondholder receive?
   - $700
   - $30
   - $3,000
   - [x] $300
   > Recovery = $1,000 × 0.30 = $300.
