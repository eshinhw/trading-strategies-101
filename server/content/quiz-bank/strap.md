# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a Strap: buys two $100 calls and buys one $100 put, for a net debit of $9 per share. What is the lower break-even stock price at expiration, per share?
   - $104.50
   - [x] $91
   - $9
   - $0
   > For this position (K = 100, net debit = 9), the lower break-even stock price is $91 per share. Formula: S*_up = K + D/2; S*_down = K - D.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A trader builds a Strap: buys two $100 calls and buys one $100 put, for a net debit of $9 per share. At expiration the stock is at $92. What is the trader's profit or loss per share, counting the premium?
   - +$1
   - [x] −$1
   - +$8
   - +$17
   > At $92 the option legs are worth +$8 per share before the premium, and the net premium adds −$9, for −$1 per share (given K = 100, net debit = 9).

5. {#bk5} [calc] A trader builds a Strap: buys two $100 calls and buys one $100 put, for a net debit of $9 per share. What is the maximum loss on 5 contracts (100 shares each), in dollars?
   - [x] $4,500
   - $45
   - $900
   - $5,400
   > The maximum loss is $9 per share. One contract covers 100 shares, so 5 contracts give $9 × 100 × 5 = $4,500.
