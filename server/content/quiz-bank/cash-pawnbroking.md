# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A pawnbroker lends $200 against a watch and charges 15% a month in simple interest. How much must the borrower repay to redeem it after 2 months?
   - $230
   - $266
   - $300
   - [x] $260
   > Interest is $200 × 15% × 2 = $60, so the borrower repays $200 + $60 = $260.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A pawnshop lends $150 on an item it values at $400. If the loan is not repaid and the shop sells the item for $400, what is its gross profit over the loan?
   - $150
   - [x] $250
   - $400
   - $550
   > The shop recovers $400 and lent $150, for a $250 gross profit before interest and costs.
