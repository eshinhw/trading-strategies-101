# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A plan needs approval by at least two-thirds in amount of a class holding $240 million of claims. How much must an investor hold to be able to block it?
   - [x] More than $80 million
   - More than $120 million
   - More than $160 million
   - $240 million
   > Blocking needs more than one-third: $240 million / 3 = $80 million, so just over $80 million.

2. {#bk2} [calc] An investor buys $80 million of face value of a class at 45 cents to gain a blocking position. What does the stake cost?
   - $80 million
   - $44 million
   - [x] $36 million
   - $3.6 million
   > Cost = $80 million × 0.45 = $36 million.
