# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An ETF trades at $100.30 while its intraday indicative value is $100.10. A creation unit is 50,000 shares. Ignoring costs, what is the arbitrage profit from selling the ETF and buying the basket?
   - $0.20
   - $5,015,000
   - $1,000
   - [x] $10,000
   > The gap is $0.20 per share. $0.20 × 50,000 = $10,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] An ETF trades $0.20 above its indicative value. Trading costs total $0.05 per share on a 50,000 share creation unit. What is the net profit?
   - $10,000
   - [x] $7,500
   - $2,500
   - $12,500
   > Net gap = $0.20 − $0.05 = $0.15. $0.15 × 50,000 = $7,500.

3. {#bk3} [calc] An ETF trades at $50.25 against an indicative value of $50.00. What is the premium?
   - 0.25%
   - 5%
   - 50%
   - [x] 0.5%
   > ($50.25 − $50.00) / $50.00 = 0.5%.
