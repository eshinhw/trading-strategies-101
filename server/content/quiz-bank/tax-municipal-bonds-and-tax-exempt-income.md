# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A municipal bond yields 3.5% tax-free. For an investor in the 35% tax bracket, what taxable yield is equivalent?
   - [x] 5.38%
   - 2.28%
   - 4.73%
   - 2.69%
   > Taxable-equivalent yield = 3.5% / (1 − 0.35) = 5.38%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A municipal bond yields 3% tax-free. For an investor in the 24% tax bracket, what taxable yield is equivalent?
   - 2.28%
   - 3.72%
   - [x] 3.95%
   - 1.97%
   > Taxable-equivalent yield = 3% / (1 − 0.24) = 3.95%.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A municipal bond yields 4.2% tax-free. For an investor in the 37% tax bracket, what taxable yield is equivalent?
   - [x] 6.67%
   - 2.65%
   - 5.75%
   - 3.33%
   > Taxable-equivalent yield = 4.2% / (1 − 0.37) = 6.67%.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A taxable bond yields 4.5% and a municipal bond of similar risk yields 3%. At what tax rate are they equal after tax?
   - 30%
   - 66.7%
   - 1.5%
   - [x] 33.3%
   > The break-even rate solves 4.5% × (1 − t) = 3%, so t = 1 − 3/4.5 = 33.3%.
