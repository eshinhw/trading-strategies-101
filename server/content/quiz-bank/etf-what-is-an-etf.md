# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An ETF has $480 million in assets, $12 million in liabilities, and 9.36 million shares outstanding. What is its net asset value per share?
   - [x] $50.00
   - $51.28
   - $48.72
   - $5.00
   > NAV = (assets − liabilities) / shares = ($480 million − $12 million) / 9.36 million = $50.00.
