# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a Short Combo: buys one $95 put and sells one $105 call, for no net premium. What is the break-even stock price at expiration, per share?
   - $10
   - $96
   - $94
   - [x] $95
   > For this position (K1 = 95, K2 = 105), the break-even stock price is $95 per share. Formula: S* = K1 + netCF (if netCF<0); S* = K2 + netCF (if netCF>0).

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader builds a Short Combo: buys one $95 put and sells one $105 call, for no net premium. What is the maximum profit on 4 contracts (100 shares each), in dollars?
   - $380
   - [x] $38,000
   - $9,500
   - $47,500
   > The maximum profit is $95 per share. One contract covers 100 shares, so 4 contracts give $95 × 100 × 4 = $38,000.
