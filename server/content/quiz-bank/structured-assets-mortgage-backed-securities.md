# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A pool of mortgages has a 6.0% weighted average coupon, and 0.5% goes to servicing and guarantee fees. For a $120 million balance, what monthly interest is passed through to investors?
   - [x] $550,000
   - $600,000
   - $5,500,000
   - $660,000
   > The pass-through rate is 6.0% − 0.5% = 5.5%. Monthly interest = $120 million × 5.5% / 12 = $550,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A borrower takes a $300,000 30-year mortgage at 6% interest. What is the monthly payment (principal and interest)?
   - $1,500.00
   - $1,000.00
   - [x] $1,798.65
   - $2,250.00
   > Monthly rate is 0.5% over 360 payments: $300,000 × 0.005 / (1 − 1.005⁻³⁶⁰) = $1,798.65.
