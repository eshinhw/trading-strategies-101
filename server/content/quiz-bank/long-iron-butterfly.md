# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a "Long" Iron Butterfly: buys one $90 put, sells one $100 put, sells one $100 call, and buys one $110 call, for a net credit of $6 per share. What is the maximum profit at expiration, per share?
   - $4
   - [x] $6
   - $20
   - $94
   > For this position (K1 = 90, K2 = 100, K3 = 110, net credit = 6), the maximum profit is $6 per share. Formula: P_max = C.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader builds a "Long" Iron Butterfly: buys one $90 put, sells one $100 put, sells one $100 call, and buys one $110 call, for a net credit of $6 per share. What is the lower break-even stock price at expiration, per share?
   - $106
   - $20
   - $6
   - [x] $94
   > For this position (K1 = 90, K2 = 100, K3 = 110, net credit = 6), the lower break-even stock price is $94 per share. Formula: S*_up = K2 + C; S*_down = K2 - C.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A trader builds a "Long" Iron Butterfly: buys one $90 put, sells one $100 put, sells one $100 call, and buys one $110 call, for a net credit of $6 per share. At expiration the stock is at $103. What is the trader's profit or loss per share, counting the premium?
   - −$3
   - +$15
   - [x] +$3
   - +$4
   > At $103 the option legs are worth −$3 per share before the premium, and the net premium adds +$6, for +$3 per share (given K1 = 90, K2 = 100, K3 = 110, net credit = 6).

6. {#bk6} [calc] A trader builds a "Long" Iron Butterfly: buys one $90 put, sells one $100 put, sells one $100 call, and buys one $110 call, for a net credit of $6 per share. What is the maximum profit on 10 contracts (100 shares each), in dollars?
   - $60
   - $600
   - [x] $6,000
   - $6,600
   > The maximum profit is $6 per share. One contract covers 100 shares, so 10 contracts give $6 × 100 × 10 = $6,000.
