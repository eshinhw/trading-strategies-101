# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] The VIX closes at 24. What one-standard-deviation move in the S&P 500 does that imply over the next month?
   - [x] 6.93%
   - 1.51%
   - 2.00%
   - 24.00%
   > The VIX is an annualized volatility. For one month, divide by √12: 24% / 3.464 = 6.93%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] With the S&P 500 at 4,500 and the VIX at 18, what is the approximate one-standard-deviation range for the next month, in index points?
   - ±810 points
   - ±51 points
   - [x] ±234 points
   - ±468 points
   > One-month move = 4,500 × 18% / √12 = 234 points.
