# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] Spot oil is $75, the 3-month future is $77, and the 6-month future is $79. What shape is the curve, and what is the 6-month annualized slope relative to spot?
   - Backwardation, about 10.7%
   - [x] Contango, about 10.7%
   - Contango, about 5.3%
   - Backwardation, about 5.3%
   > Futures above spot is contango. The 6-month slope is ($79 − $75) / $75 = 5.3% over six months, or about 10.7% annualized.

2. {#bk2} [calc] Natural gas spot is $4.00 and the 3-month future is $3.70. What is the 3-month basis (spot minus futures) and the curve shape?
   - $0.30, contango
   - −$0.30, backwardation
   - $7.70, contango
   - [x] $0.30, backwardation
   > Spot minus futures is $4.00 − $3.70 = $0.30. Futures below spot is backwardation.

3. {#bk3} [calc] Gold spot is $1,950 and the 1-year future is $2,040. Assuming the gap is pure cost of carry, what is the implied annual carry cost?
   - 9.0%
   - [x] 4.6%
   - 0.46%
   - 2.3%
   > ($2,040 − $1,950) / $1,950 = 4.6% a year of financing and storage.
