# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A foreign dividend of $1,000,000 faces 30% withholding. Routing the holding through a treaty structure lowers withholding to 15%. How much tax is saved?
   - [x] $150,000
   - $300,000
   - $450,000
   - $15,000
   > Saving = (30% − 15%) × $1,000,000 = $150,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A structure saves $150,000 of withholding tax and costs 20% of the saving in fees. What is the net benefit?
   - $30,000
   - $150,000
   - [x] $120,000
   - $180,000
   > Fees = 20% × $150,000 = $30,000. Net = $150,000 − $30,000 = $120,000.
