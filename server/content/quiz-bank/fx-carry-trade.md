# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An investor borrows yen at 0.5% and invests in Australian dollars at 4.5%. If exchange rates do not change, what is the annual carry on $1 million?
   - $50,000
   - $4,000
   - [x] $40,000
   - $45,000
   > The carry is the rate difference: 4.5% − 0.5% = 4.0%, or 4.0% × $1,000,000 = $40,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A carry trade earns 4.0% in interest differential, but the high-yield currency falls 3% against the funding currency. What is the net return?
   - [x] 1%
   - 7%
   - 4%
   - −3%
   > Net return ≈ interest differential minus currency loss = 4% − 3% = 1%.
