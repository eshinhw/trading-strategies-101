# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A fund holds the front crude oil future at 80 and rolls it into the next contract at 82 with spot unchanged. What is the roll return?
   - [x] −2.50%
   - 2.50%
   - 2.00%
   - −30.00%
   > Selling at 80 and buying at 82 costs (80 − 82) / 80 = −2.50% in contango.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A fund holds the front gold future at 1,950 and rolls it into the next contract at 1,962 with spot unchanged. What is the roll return?
   - 0.62%
   - 12.00%
   - [x] −0.62%
   - −7.38%
   > Selling at 1,950 and buying at 1,962 costs (1,950 − 1,962) / 1,950 = −0.62% in contango.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A fund rolls from a front future at $100 into a next contract at $98 (backwardation), with spot unchanged, every month. What is the monthly roll yield?
   - [x] +2%
   - −2%
   - +2.04%
   - 0%
   > Selling at $100 and buying at $98 earns $2 on $100, a +2% roll yield per month.
