# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A stock closes at 50, 51, 53, 54 and 56 over five days. What are the 3-day and 5-day simple moving averages on the last day, and which is higher?
   - 3-day 52.80 and 5-day 54.33, so the slow average is higher
   - Both equal 52.80
   - [x] 3-day 54.33 and 5-day 52.80, so the fast average is higher
   - 3-day 54.33 and 5-day 54.33
   > The 3-day average is (53 + 54 + 56) / 3 = 54.33. The 5-day average is (50 + 51 + 53 + 54 + 56) / 5 = 52.80. The fast average is above the slow one, a bullish crossover.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A crossover system trades 12 round trips a year with costs of 0.15% per round trip. What is the annual cost drag?
   - [x] 1.8%
   - 0.15%
   - 15%
   - 0.18%
   > 12 × 0.15% = 1.8% a year.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A crossover strategy has 10 trades: 4 winners averaging +6% and 6 losers averaging −2%. What is the average return per trade?
   - +4.0%
   - +2.0%
   - [x] +1.2%
   - −0.4%
   > Total = 4 × 6% − 6 × 2% = 24% − 12% = 12%. Average = 12% / 10 = +1.2% per trade.
