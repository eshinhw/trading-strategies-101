# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] Nominal GDP grew 6.2% while inflation was 2.4%. What was the approximate real GDP growth?
   - [x] 3.8%
   - 8.6%
   - 2.6%
   - 14.9%
   > Real growth ≈ nominal growth − inflation = 6.2% − 2.4% = 3.8%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A country has 160 million in its labor force, of whom 8 million are unemployed. What is the unemployment rate?
   - 4.8%
   - 8%
   - [x] 5%
   - 20%
   > Unemployment rate = unemployed / labor force = 8 / 160 = 5%.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A bond yields 4.5% while inflation is 3.1%. What is the approximate real yield?
   - [x] 1.4%
   - 7.6%
   - 3.1%
   - 4.5%
   > Real yield ≈ nominal yield − inflation = 4.5% − 3.1% = 1.4%.
