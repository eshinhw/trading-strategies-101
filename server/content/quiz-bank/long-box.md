# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A long box uses strikes of $90 and $100 and pays off $10 at expiration whatever the stock does. A trader buys the box for $9.60. What is the profit per share at expiration?
   - [x] +$0.40
   - +$10.00
   - −$0.40
   - +$9.60
   > The box pays $10.00 for sure. Profit = $10.00 − $9.60 = +$0.40 per share.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader buys a $90/$100 box for $9.60 that pays $10 in 6 months. What is the simple annualized return?
   - 4.17%
   - 10.4%
   - [x] 8.33%
   - 0.4%
   > Return over 6 months = $10 / $9.60 − 1 = 4.17%. Annualized: 4.17% × 2 = 8.33%.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A trader buys 20 $90/$100 boxes (100 shares each) at $9.60 each, paying $10 at expiration. What is the total profit?
   - [x] $800
   - $8
   - $8,000
   - $200
   > Profit = ($10.00 − $9.60) × 100 × 20 = $800.
