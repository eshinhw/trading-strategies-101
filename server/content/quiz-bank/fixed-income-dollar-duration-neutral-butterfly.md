# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A butterfly is long $10 million of 2-year notes (DV01 $190 per $1 million) and $4 million of 10-year notes (DV01 $850 per $1 million). What face amount of 5-year notes (DV01 $460 per $1 million) must be sold to make the position DV01-neutral?
   - $14.00 million
   - $5.00 million
   - [x] $11.52 million
   - $6.24 million
   > The wings carry DV01 of 10 × $190 + 4 × $850 = $5,300. Selling $5,300 / $460 = $11.52 million of 5-year notes offsets it.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A butterfly spread is defined as 2 × body yield − short-wing yield − long-wing yield. The 2-year yields 3.00%, the 5-year yields 4.20% and the 10-year yields 5.00%. What is the butterfly spread?
   - [x] 0.40%
   - −0.40%
   - 1.20%
   - 0.20%
   > 2 × 4.20% − 3.00% − 5.00% = 8.40% − 8.00% = 0.40%.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A dollar-duration-neutral butterfly is short the body with a DV01 of $5,000 per basis point. The butterfly spread falls 6 basis points. If the position profits from a fall in the spread at its full DV01 per basis point, what is the gain?
   - $5,000
   - $300,000
   - [x] $30,000
   - $833
   > Gain = $5,000 × 6 = $30,000.
