# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A butterfly is long $10 million of 2-year notes (DV01 $190 per $1 million) and $4 million of 10-year notes (DV01 $850 per $1 million). What face amount of 5-year notes (DV01 $460 per $1 million) must be sold to make the position DV01-neutral?
   - $14.00 million
   - $5.00 million
   - [x] $11.52 million
   - $6.24 million
   > The wings carry DV01 of 10 × $190 + 4 × $850 = $5,300. Selling $5,300 / $460 = $11.52 million of 5-year notes offsets it.
