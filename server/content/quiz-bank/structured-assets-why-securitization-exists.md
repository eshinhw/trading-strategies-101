# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A pool has 100 loans of $1 million each. Each has a 2% chance of default and 60% loss given default. What is the expected loss on the pool?
   - $2 million
   - [x] $1.2 million
   - $0.6 million
   - $12 million
   > Expected defaults are 100 × 2% = 2 loans. Expected loss = 2 × $1 million × 60% = $1.2 million.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A $500 million pool is divided so the senior tranche attaches at 20% subordination. How much of pool loss can occur before senior investors lose anything?
   - $20 million
   - $400 million
   - $500 million
   - [x] $100 million
   > Subordinated tranches below the senior tranche total 20% × $500 million = $100 million of protection.
