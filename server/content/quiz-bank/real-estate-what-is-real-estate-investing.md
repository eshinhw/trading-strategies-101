# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A rental property costs $400,000 and collects $32,000 of rent a year. What is the gross rental yield?
   - 12.5%
   - 3.2%
   - [x] 8%
   - 32%
   > $32,000 / $400,000 = 8%.

   - [x] $250
   - $25
   - $1,000
   - $2,500
   > $500,000 / 2,000 = $250.
