# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader sells protection on a $10 million mezzanine tranche at 600 basis points and buys protection on $25 million of senior index exposure at 120 basis points. What is the net annual carry?
   - [x] $300,000
   - $900,000
   - $600,000
   - $1.2 million
   > Income = 6% × $10 million = $600,000. Cost = 1.2% × $25 million = $300,000. Net = $300,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A mezzanine tranche earns 600 basis points on $10 million. The hedge costs $300,000 a year. If the tranche suffers a $450,000 loss in a year, what is the net result including the hedge cost?
   - +$150,000
   - −$450,000
   - [x] −$150,000
   - +$600,000
   > Income $600,000 − hedge cost $300,000 − loss $450,000 = −$150,000.
