# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] One crude oil future covers 1,000 barrels at $80. What is the notional value, and what is the margin if the exchange requires 8%?
   - $80,000 notional, $640 margin
   - $8,000 notional, $640 margin
   - $80,000 notional, $64,000 margin
   - [x] $80,000 notional, $6,400 margin
   > Notional is 1,000 × $80 = $80,000. Margin at 8% is 0.08 × $80,000 = $6,400.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader holds one crude oil future (1,000 barrels) with $6,400 margin. The price drops $3.20. What percentage of margin is lost?
   - 5%
   - [x] 50%
   - 3.2%
   - 25%
   > The loss is $3.20 × 1,000 = $3,200, which is $3,200 / $6,400 = 50% of the margin.
