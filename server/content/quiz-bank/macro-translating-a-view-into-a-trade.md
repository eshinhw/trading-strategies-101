# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader expects the dollar to rise 2% against the euro and risks 1% of a $1 million account on a trade with a 1% stop-loss. What position size should be used?
   - $10,000
   - [x] $1,000,000
   - $100,000
   - $2,000,000
   > Risk budget is 1% × $1,000,000 = $10,000. With a 1% stop, size = $10,000 / 0.01 = $1,000,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trade has an expected gain of 3% and a stop-loss of 1.5%. What is the reward-to-risk ratio?
   - 0.5 to 1
   - 4.5 to 1
   - 1.5 to 1
   - [x] 2 to 1
   > Reward-to-risk = 3% / 1.5% = 2.
