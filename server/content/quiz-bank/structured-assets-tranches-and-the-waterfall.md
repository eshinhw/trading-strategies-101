# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A $100 million loan pool is split into a $5 million equity tranche, a $15 million mezzanine tranche, and an $80 million senior tranche. The pool suffers $8 million of losses. What percentage of the mezzanine tranche is lost?
   - 53%
   - 8%
   - 0%
   - [x] 20%
   > The equity tranche absorbs the first $5 million. The remaining $3 million hits the mezzanine tranche: $3 million / $15 million = 20%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A $100 million loan pool is split into a $5 million equity tranche, a $15 million mezzanine tranche, and an $80 million senior tranche. The pool suffers $3 million of losses. What percentage of the equity tranche is lost, and does the senior tranche lose anything?
   - 3%, and the senior tranche loses 3%
   - [x] 60%, and the senior tranche loses nothing
   - 60%, and the senior tranche loses 3%
   - 100%, and the senior tranche loses nothing
   > Losses hit the equity tranche first: $3 million / $5 million = 60%. The senior tranche sits at the top of the waterfall and loses nothing.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A $100 million loan pool is split into a $5 million equity tranche, a $15 million mezzanine tranche, and an $80 million senior tranche. The pool suffers $22 million of losses. What percentage of the senior tranche is lost?
   - 22%
   - 27.5%
   - 0%
   - [x] 2.5%
   > Equity ($5 million) and mezzanine ($15 million) absorb $20 million. The senior tranche loses the remaining $2 million: $2 million / $80 million = 2.5%.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A $100 million loan pool is split into a $5 million equity tranche, a $15 million mezzanine tranche, and an $80 million senior tranche. The pool earns $6 million of interest. The senior tranche is owed 4% ($3.2 million) and the mezzanine tranche 8% ($1.2 million). What does the equity tranche receive, and what is its yield?
   - [x] $1.6 million, a 32% yield
   - $6 million, a 120% yield
   - $1.6 million, a 1.6% yield
   - $0.8 million, a 16% yield
   > After paying $3.2 million and $1.2 million, $6 million − $4.4 million = $1.6 million remains for equity. $1.6 million / $5 million = 32%.
