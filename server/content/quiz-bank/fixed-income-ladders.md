# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A ladder holds $1 million in each of five bonds maturing in 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 years. What is its average maturity?
   - 5 years
   - 2.5 years
   - [x] 3 years
   - 15 years
   > The average of 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 is 3 years, with equal weights.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A ladder holds $1 million in each of five bonds yielding 3.0%, 3.5%, 4.0%, 4.5% and 5.0%. What is the annual interest income?
   - [x] $200,000
   - $40,000
   - $20,000
   - $1,000,000
   > Income = $1 million × (3.0% + 3.5% + 4.0% + 4.5% + 5.0%) = $1 million × 20% = $200,000.

3. {#bk3} [calc] When the 1-year rung matures, a ladder holder reinvests in a new 5-year bond. The rung yields were 3.5%, 4.0%, 4.5% and 5.0% (remaining rungs) and the new 5-year bond yields 5.2%. What is the ladder's equal-weighted yield now?
   - 5.20%
   - 4.25%
   - [x] 4.44%
   - 4.00%
   > Average = (3.5 + 4.0 + 4.5 + 5.0 + 5.2) / 5 = 22.2 / 5 = 4.44%.
