# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A $100 million pool has a 12% annualized prepayment rate (CPR). What is the approximate prepayment for one month (SMM)?
   - $12.00 million
   - $1.00 million
   - [x] $1.06 million
   - $0.12 million
   > SMM = 1 − (1 − 0.12)^(1/12) = 1.06%. On $100 million that is $1.06 million, slightly more than 1/12 of 12%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A mortgage pass-through bought at 102 has a 5% coupon. When 20% of the pool prepays at par, what is the loss on the prepaid part, per $100 of original face?
   - [x] $0.40
   - $2.00
   - $0.20
   - $0.10
   > Each prepaid $1 is returned at par, losing the 2-point premium. 20% × $2 = $0.40 per $100 of original face.
