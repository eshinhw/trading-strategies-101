# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a Protective Put: owns the stock, bought at $100, and buys one $95 put, with a net option premium of $3 paid. What is the break-even stock price at expiration, per share?
   - $8
   - $3
   - $104
   - [x] $103
   > For this position (S0 = 100, K = 95, net debit = 3), the break-even stock price is $103 per share. Formula: S* = S0 + D.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader builds a Protective Put: owns the stock, bought at $100, and buys one $95 put, with a net option premium of $3 paid. At expiration the stock is at $87. What is the trader's profit or loss per share, counting the premium?
   - +$8
   - [x] −$8
   - −$5
   - −$2
   > At $87 the option legs are worth −$5 per share before the premium, and the net premium adds −$3, for −$8 per share (given S0 = 100, K = 95, net debit = 3).

4. {#bk4} [calc] A trader builds a Protective Put: owns the stock, bought at $100, and buys one $95 put, with a net option premium of $3 paid. What is the maximum loss on 4 contracts (100 shares each), in dollars?
