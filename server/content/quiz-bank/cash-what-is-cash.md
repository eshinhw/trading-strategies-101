# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An account holds $10,000 earning 3% a year while inflation is 4%. What is the real return, and how much purchasing power is lost on the balance?
   - [x] About −1%, a loss of about $100
   - About +1%, a gain of about $100
   - About −7%, a loss of about $700
   - About 0%, no change
   > Real return ≈ 3% − 4% = −1%. On $10,000 that is about −$100 of purchasing power.
