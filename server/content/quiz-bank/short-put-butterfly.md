# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a Short Put Butterfly: sells one $110 put, buys two $100 puts, and sells one $90 put, for a net credit of $3 per share. What is the maximum loss at expiration, per share?
   - $3
   - [x] $7
   - $20
   - $93
   > For this position (K1 = 110, K2 = 100, K3 = 90, net credit = 3), the maximum loss is $7 per share. Formula: L_max = κ - C, where κ = K1-K2 = K2-K3.
