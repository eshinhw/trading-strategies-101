# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An ETF's creation unit is 50,000 shares and each share's net asset value is $40. What is the value of one creation unit?
   - $200,000
   - $20,000,000
   - [x] $2,000,000
   - $50,000
   > Creation unit value = 50,000 × $40 = $2,000,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] An ETF has a net asset value of $100 per share and trades at $100.50. What is the premium or discount?
   - [x] 0.50% premium
   - 0.50% discount
   - 5.00% premium
   - 0.25% premium
   > ($100.50 − $100) / $100 = +0.50%, a premium.

3. {#bk3} [calc] An ETF has a net asset value of $50 per share and trades at $49.40. What is the premium or discount?
   - 1.20% premium
   - 12.00% discount
   - [x] 1.20% discount
   - 0.60% discount
   > ($49.40 − $50) / $50 = −1.20%, a discount.

4. {#bk4} [calc] An ETF has a net asset value of $25 per share and trades at $25.30. What is the premium or discount?
   - 1.20% discount
   - [x] 1.20% premium
   - 12.00% premium
   - 0.60% premium
   > ($25.30 − $25) / $25 = +1.20%, a premium.

5. {#bk5} [calc] An authorized participant sees an ETF trading at $50.40 while its basket is worth $50.00 per share. The creation unit is 100,000 shares. Ignoring costs, what is the arbitrage profit from creating and selling a unit?
   - $4,000
   - $400,000
   - $50,400
   - [x] $40,000
   > The participant buys the basket at $50.00, delivers it for new ETF shares, and sells them at $50.40, earning $0.40 × 100,000 = $40,000.
