# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A stock is $50, the interest rate is 5% and the dividend yield is 2% (continuous). What is the fair 1-year forward price?
   - $52.56
   - $50.00
   - $48.51
   - [x] $51.52
   > F = $50 × e^((5% − 2%) × 1) = $51.52.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A bond costs $980 and pays a $40 coupon in 3 months. The 6-month forward is priced with a 5% continuous rate. What is the fair forward price?
   - $1,004.77
   - [x] $964.31
   - $980.00
   - $940.00
   > PV of the coupon = $40 × e^(−0.05 × 0.25) = $39.50. Forward = ($980 − $39.50) × e^(0.05 × 0.5) = $964.31.
