# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] In an inflation swap on $10 million, the fixed rate is 2.5% and realized inflation is 3.2%. What does the inflation receiver gain?
   - $320,000
   - $250,000
   - [x] $70,000
   - $700,000
   > The receiver gets realized minus fixed: (3.2% − 2.5%) × $10 million = $70,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] An inflation swap on $50 million has a fixed rate of 2.8% and realized inflation of 2.1%. What does the inflation receiver pay?
   - [x] $350,000
   - $1,050,000
   - $35,000
   - $3,500,000
   > The receiver loses (2.1% − 2.8%) × $50 million = −$350,000, so it pays $350,000.
