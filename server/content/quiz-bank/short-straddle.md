# Quiz

1. {#q1} When might a trader sell a straddle?
   - A big announcement is coming and the move is expected to be large, so the premium is attractive to collect
   - The trader is sure that the stock will rise and wants to be paid for waiting while it climbs higher
   - [x] The stock is expected to stay close to its current price with low volatility, for example after a catalyst has passed
   - The trader wants limited, known risk and is willing to accept a small profit in exchange for it
   > A short straddle profits when the stock does not move much.

2. {#q2} What is the main risk of a short straddle?
   - The profit is unlimited but the loss is limited, so the trade is considered attractive to most investors
   - The loss is limited to the premium collected, because both options are sold at the same strike
   - There is no risk if the stock stays above the strike, since the call premium covers any loss on the put
   - [x] A large move in either direction produces large, technically unlimited, losses
   > The trader is explicitly betting against a big move, and the loss grows with it.

3. {#q3} How is a short straddle built?
   - [x] Sell an at-the-money call and an at-the-money put with the same strike and expiration
   - Buy a call and a put at the same strike and expiration, paying a premium for both options up front
   - Sell an out-of-the-money call and put at different strikes, which widens the range of the profit zone
   - Sell a call and buy a put at the same strike and expiration, so that the position behaves like shorting
   > Two premiums are collected from the same strike.

4. {#q4} A stock jumps 30% on surprise news after a short straddle is opened. What happens?
   - The trader keeps the full credit, because the short call and the short put cancel each other's losses
   - [x] The loss is large and grows the further the stock moves
   - The loss is limited to the premium collected, because the position is protected by the opposite option
   - The trader profits from the short put
   > A short straddle has no protection against large moves.
