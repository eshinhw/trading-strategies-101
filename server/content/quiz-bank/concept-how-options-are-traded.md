# Quiz

1. {#q1} What does the Options Clearing Corporation do?
   - It sets the strike prices
   - [x] It stands behind every contract, guaranteeing performance so buyers don't rely on the seller
   - It chooses which stocks have options
   - It pays dividends
   > A clearinghouse guarantees each listed contract, which lets options be traded freely.

2. {#q2} [calc] A listed option is quoted at 3.00. How much does one contract cost before commission?
   - 3 dollars
   - 30 dollars
   - [x] 300 dollars
   - 3,000 dollars
   > One contract covers 100 shares, so the cost is 3.00 × 100 = 300 dollars.

3. {#q3} What does open interest tell you?
   - The interest rate on the option
   - [x] The number of contracts currently outstanding
   - The number traded today
   - The option's profit
   > Open interest counts the open contracts, and along with volume it indicates how easy an option is to trade.

4. {#q4} What can happen to an option seller if the buyer exercises?
   - Nothing at all
   - The seller receives extra premium
   - [x] The seller can be assigned and must deliver the shares or the cash value
   - The contract is cancelled
   > Assignment obliges the seller to fulfill the contract at the strike price.

5. {#q5} [calc] A call has a bid of 3.90 and an ask of 4.10. What is the cost of buying and immediately selling one contract?
   - 0 dollars
   - 4 dollars
   - [x] About 20 dollars, from the gap between ask and bid
   - 410 dollars
   > You pay 4.10 and receive 3.90, a difference of 0.20 per share, or 20 dollars per contract.
