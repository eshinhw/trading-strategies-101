# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A macro fund is long a currency that gains 4% and short a bond that loses 3% in price, each on equal $10 million positions. What is the total profit?
   - [x] $700,000
   - $100,000
   - $70,000
   - $7,000,000
   > The long earns 4% × $10 million = $400,000 and the short earns 3% × $10 million = $300,000. Total = $700,000.
