# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A bond yields 4.8% and the investor's funding cost is 3.0%. What is the carry?
   - 7.8%
   - [x] 1.8%
   - 3.0%
   - 4.8%
   > Carry = yield − funding cost = 4.8% − 3.0% = 1.8%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A bond yields 4.8%, funding costs 3.0%, and roll-down adds 0.4% a year. What is the total expected return from carry and roll-down?
   - 1.8%
   - 0.4%
   - 8.2%
   - [x] 2.2%
   > Total = (4.8% − 3.0%) + 0.4% = 2.2%.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A carry strategy holds $50 million of bonds yielding 4.8% funded at 3.0%. What is the annual carry in dollars?
   - $2,400,000
   - [x] $900,000
   - $1,500,000
   - $90,000
   > Carry = ($50 million) × (4.8% − 3.0%) = $50 million × 1.8% = $900,000.
