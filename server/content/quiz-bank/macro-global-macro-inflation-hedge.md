# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A nominal 10-year Treasury yields 4.4% and a 10-year TIPS yields 2.0%. What is the breakeven inflation rate?
   - 6.4%
   - 2.0%
   - [x] 2.4%
   - 4.4%
   > Breakeven inflation = nominal yield − real yield = 4.4% − 2.0% = 2.4%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A $1,000 TIPS principal is adjusted for 3% inflation over the year. What is the adjusted principal?
   - [x] $1,030
   - $1,003
   - $970
   - $1,300
   > $1,000 × 1.03 = $1,030.
