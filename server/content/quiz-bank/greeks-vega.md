# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An option has a vega of $0.12 per share for each 1-point rise in implied volatility. If implied volatility rises 5 points, how much does the option gain per share?
   - [x] $0.60
   - $0.12
   - $6.00
   - $0.024
   > Gain = $0.12 × 5 = $0.60 per share.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader is short 10 option contracts (100 shares each) with a vega of $0.15 per point. Implied volatility falls 3 points. What is the trader's gain?
   - $45
   - $4,500
   - [x] $450
   - $150
   > The short position gains when volatility falls: $0.15 × 3 × 100 × 10 = $450.
