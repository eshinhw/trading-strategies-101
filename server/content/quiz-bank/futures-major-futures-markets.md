# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader buys 7 gold contracts (100 ounces each) at 1,960, and the price later moves to 2,004. What is the profit or loss on the position?
   - +$308
   - +$4,400
   - [x] +$30,800
   - −$30,800
   > The move is 2,004 − 1,960 = 44.00 per unit, and 44.00 × 100 × 7 contracts = $30,800.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader buys 2 copper contracts (25,000 pounds each) at 4.10, and the price later moves to 4.34. What is the profit or loss on the position?
   - [x] +$12,000
   - +$0.48
   - +$6,000
   - −$12,000
   > The move is 4.34 − 4.10 = 0.24 per unit, and 0.24 × 25,000 × 2 contracts = $12,000.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A gold future covers 100 ounces and gold is at $2,000. What is the contract's notional value?
   - $2,000
   - $20,000
   - [x] $200,000
   - $2,000,000
   > Notional = price × contract size = $2,000 × 100 = $200,000.

4. {#bk4} [calc] The E-mini S&P 500 has a multiplier of $50 and the Micro E-mini has a multiplier of $5. How many micro contracts equal the exposure of one E-mini?
   - 5
   - [x] 10
   - 50
   - 100
   > $50 / $5 = 10 micro contracts per E-mini.

5. {#bk5} [calc] A natural gas contract covers 10,000 MMBtu and gas trades at $3.40. The margin is $3,000 per contract. What is the margin as a percentage of notional?
   - 11.3%
   - 0.9%
   - 88%
   - [x] 8.8%
   > Notional = 10,000 × $3.40 = $34,000. Margin / notional = $3,000 / $34,000 = 8.8%.

6. {#bk6} [calc] A crude oil contract (1,000 barrels) is at $80 with $6,400 of margin. What leverage does the margin give (notional divided by margin)?
   - 8 times
   - [x] 12.5 times
   - 80 times
   - 1.25 times
   > Notional = $80,000. Leverage = $80,000 / $6,400 = 12.5 times.
