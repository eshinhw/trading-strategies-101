# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader is long a forward for 10,000 bushels of wheat at $6.50. At delivery the spot price is $7.20. What is the payoff on the long forward?
   - −$7,000
   - $1
   - [x] +$7,000
   - $72,000
   > The long gains the amount spot finishes above the delivery price: ($7.20 − $6.50) × 10,000 = $7,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader is long a forward for 1,000 barrels of oil at $85. At delivery the spot price is $78. What is the payoff on the long forward?
   - [x] −$7,000
   - +$7,000
   - $7
   - $78,000
   > The long gains the amount spot finishes above the delivery price: ($78 − $85) × 1,000 = −$7,000.
