# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader buys December crude at 78.40 and sells March crude at 80.15 (1,000 barrels per contract). Later December trades at 79.30 and March at 80.20. What is the profit on 5 spreads?
   - $850
   - $5,000
   - $850,000
   - [x] $4,250
   > The spread (December − March) moved from −1.75 to −0.90, a gain of 0.85 per barrel. 0.85 × 1,000 × 5 = $4,250.

2. {#bk2} [calc] Spot gold is $80 and the 3-month future is $82.40. Financing costs $1.00 and storage costs $0.60 over the three months. How far is the future from full cost of carry?
   - $0.80 below
   - [x] $0.80 above
   - $1.60 above
   - $2.40 above
   > Full carry is $80 + $1.00 + $0.60 = $81.60. The future at $82.40 is $0.80 above it, so a cash-and-carry arbitrage may exist.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A calendar spread is long the near contract at 4.10 and short the far contract at 4.45, 5,000 units each. The spread widens so the far contract is 0.20 more expensive relative to the near. What is the loss on 2 spreads?
   - $1,000
   - $200
   - $20,000
   - [x] $2,000
   > The spread loses 0.20 per unit. 0.20 × 5,000 × 2 = $2,000.
