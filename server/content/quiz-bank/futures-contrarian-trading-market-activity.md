# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] Volume in a futures market is 120,000 contracts while its 20-day average is 80,000. What percentage of the average is today's volume?
   - 67%
   - 50%
   - 40%
   - [x] 150%
   > 120,000 / 80,000 = 1.5, or 150% of average, a volume spike.

2. {#bk2} [calc] Open interest in a futures contract rises from 410,000 to 451,000 while the price climbs steadily. By what percentage did open interest increase?
   - 9.1%
   - [x] 10%
   - 41,000%
   - 0.1%
   > (451,000 − 410,000) / 410,000 = 10%. Rising open interest means new money is entering the trend.

3. {#bk3} [calc] Daily volume is 90,000 contracts and open interest is 450,000. What is the volume-to-open-interest ratio?
   - 5×
   - 50%
   - 0.2%
   - [x] 20%
   > 90,000 / 450,000 = 0.20, or 20%. A very high ratio can signal short-term exhaustion.
