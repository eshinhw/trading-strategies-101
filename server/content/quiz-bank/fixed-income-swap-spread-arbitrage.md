# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A 10-year swap rate is 4.35% and the 10-year Treasury yield is 4.10%. A trader long the swap spread has a position DV01 of $8,500 per basis point, and the spread widens by 6 basis points. What is the gain?
   - $510
   - [x] $51,000
   - $212,500
   - $263,500
   > The spread starts at 4.35% − 4.10% = 25 basis points. The gain is the DV01 times the move: $8,500 × 6 = $51,000.
