# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A convertible's straight-bond spread is 250 basis points and its embedded option is worth 180 basis points of spread. What is the option-adjusted spread?
   - [x] 70 basis points
   - 430 basis points
   - 250 basis points
   - 180 basis points
   > OAS = bond spread − option value in spread terms = 250 − 180 = 70 basis points.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A convertible's bond spread is 250 basis points and its option is worth 180. After a rally the option is worth 210 basis points. What happens to the option-adjusted spread?
   - It rises to 460 basis points
   - It stays at 70 basis points
   - [x] It falls to 40 basis points
   - It falls to 210 basis points
   > OAS = 250 − 210 = 40 basis points. The convertible looks richer as the option gains value.
