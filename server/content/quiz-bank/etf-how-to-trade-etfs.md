# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An ETF trades at a $0.04 bid-ask spread and a $80 share price. What is the spread as a percentage of price?
   - [x] 0.05%
   - 0.5%
   - 0.04%
   - 5%
   > $0.04 / $80 = 0.0005, or 0.05% (5 basis points).

2. {#bk2} [calc] An investor places a market order for 500 ETF shares quoted $30.00 bid / $30.06 ask. How much do they pay at the quoted ask?
   - $15,000
   - $15,015
   - [x] $15,030
   - $14,970
   > A market buy fills at the ask: 500 × $30.06 = $15,030.
