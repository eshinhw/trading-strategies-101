# Quiz

1. {#q1} What is credit risk?
   - The risk that interest rates change
   - [x] The chance that the issuer fails to make its interest or principal payments
   - The risk that a bond has a long maturity
   - The risk of inflation
   > Credit risk is the risk of default, and it also covers worries about the issuer's finances that lower the bond's price.

2. {#q2} What does it mean for a bond to be investment grade?
   - It pays a coupon above 5%
   - It is issued by a government only
   - [x] It is rated BBB- (Baa3) or higher, indicating a relatively lower chance of default
   - It is rated below BBB- (Baa3)
   > Bonds rated BBB- or Baa3 and above are investment grade, and below that they are high yield.

3. {#q3} [calc] Treasury yield is 4.0% and a corporate bond of the same maturity yields 5.5%. What is the credit spread?
   - 4.0%, or 400 basis points
   - [x] 1.5%, or 150 basis points
   - 5.5%, or 550 basis points
   - 9.5%, or 950 basis points
   > The spread is the corporate yield minus the government yield: 5.5% − 4.0% = 1.5%, or 150 basis points.

4. {#q4} What typically happens to credit spreads in a crisis?
   - They narrow
   - They stay the same
   - [x] They widen, as investors demand more compensation for credit risk
   - They become negative
   > In stress, investors want more return to hold risky bonds, so spreads widen and prices fall.

5. {#q5} [calc] A bond with a duration of 5 sees its yield rise by 1.00% from a wider spread. About how much does its price fall?
   - 1%
   - 0.2%
   - 50%
   - [x] 5%
   > A bond's price change is roughly its duration times the change in yield, so 5 × 1.00% = 5%.

6. {#q6} [calc] The manufacturer's bond yields 5.0% when the 10-year Treasury yields 3.5%. What is its credit spread?
   - 1.5 basis points
   - 15 basis points
   - [x] 150 basis points
   - 350 basis points
   > The spread is 5.0% - 3.5% = 1.5%, which is 150 basis points.

7. {#q7} [calc] After a downgrade the spread widens to 250 basis points and Treasury yields are unchanged. What is the bond's new yield?
   - 5.0%
   - 5.5%
   - [x] 6.0%
   - 6.5%
   > The Treasury yield of 3.5% plus a 2.50% spread gives 6.0%, which is 1.00 percentage point higher than before, so the bond's price falls.

11. {#bk4} [calc] A bond trades at a 240 basis point credit spread. Assuming the spread only compensates for default losses and loss given default is 60%, what annual default probability does the spread imply?

   - 2.00%
