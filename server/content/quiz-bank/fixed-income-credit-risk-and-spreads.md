# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A corporate bond yields 7.25% and a Treasury of the same maturity yields 4.25%. What is the credit spread?
   - 30 basis points
   - 3000 basis points
   - [x] 300 basis points
   - 1150 basis points
   > The spread is 7.25% − 4.25% = 3.00%, or 300 basis points.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A corporate bond yields 8.50% and a Treasury of the same maturity yields 4.50%. What is the credit spread?
   - [x] 400 basis points
   - 40 basis points
   - 4000 basis points
   - 1300 basis points
   > The spread is 8.50% − 4.50% = 4.00%, or 400 basis points.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A corporate bond yields 6.20% and a Treasury of the same maturity yields 4.20%. What is the credit spread?
   - 20 basis points
   - 2000 basis points
   - [x] 200 basis points
   - 1040 basis points
   > The spread is 6.20% − 4.20% = 2.00%, or 200 basis points.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A bond trades at a 240 basis point credit spread. Assuming the spread only compensates for default losses and loss given default is 60%, what annual default probability does the spread imply?
   - 2.40%
   - [x] 4.00%
   - 8.00%
   - 2.00%
   > Implied default probability ≈ spread / loss given default = 0.0240 / 0.6 = 4.00%.

5. {#bk5} [calc] A bond trades at a 180 basis point credit spread. Assuming the spread only compensates for default losses and loss given default is 40%, what annual default probability does the spread imply?

   - [x] 4.50%
   > Implied default probability ≈ spread / loss given default = 0.0180 / 0.4 = 4.50%.
