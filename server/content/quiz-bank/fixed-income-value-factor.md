# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A bond's spread is 230 basis points and a model predicts a fair spread of 180 basis points. How cheap or rich is the bond?
   - [x] 50 basis points cheap
   - 50 basis points rich
   - 410 basis points cheap
   - 1.28 basis points cheap
   > The market spread is 50 basis points wider than fair, so the bond is cheap.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader buys a cheap bond (spread 50 basis points too wide) with a DV01 of $4,000 per basis point of spread. The spread tightens by 30 basis points. What is the gain?
   - $200,000
   - $12,000
   - [x] $120,000
   - $1,200,000
   > Gain = $4,000 × 30 = $120,000.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A bond's spread is 30 basis points wider than fair. Spreads of similar bonds have a standard deviation of 15 basis points around fair. What is the z-score?
   - [x] 2.0
   - 0.5
   - 30
   - 45
   > z = 30 / 15 = 2.0.
