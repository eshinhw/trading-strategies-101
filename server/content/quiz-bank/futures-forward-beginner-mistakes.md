# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader sells a forward on 100,000 bushels at $6.50, a $650,000 notional. The spot price rises 5% before delivery. What is the loss on the short forward?
   - $650,000
   - $3,250
   - [x] $32,500
   - $325,000
   > The loss is the price move, not the notional: 5% × $650,000 = $32,500.
