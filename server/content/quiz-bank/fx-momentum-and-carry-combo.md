# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A currency has a carry z-score of +1.2 and a momentum z-score of +0.6. The strategy gives each signal equal weight. What is the combined score?
   - [x] 0.9
   - 1.8
   - 0.6
   - 1.2
   > (1.2 + 0.6) / 2 = 0.9.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A currency's carry z-score is +1.5 and its momentum z-score is −0.5. With weights of 50% carry and 50% momentum, what is the combined score and is it a buy?
   - +1.0, a buy
   - −0.5, a sell
   - [x] +0.5, a buy
   - +0.5, a sell
   > (1.5 − 0.5) / 2 = +0.5, a positive score, so the rule buys.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A combination strategy sizes each currency at $1 million per 1.0 of combined score. A currency has a combined score of 0.9. How large is the position?
   - [x] $900,000
   - $1 million
   - $90,000
   - $9 million
   > Position = 0.9 × $1 million = $900,000.
