# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader buys one commodity at $62 and sells a related one at $65.50, then closes the trade when the gap is $2.50. What is the profit per unit?
   - [x] $1.00
   - $3.50
   - $6.00
   - $2.50
   > The spread narrowed from $65.50 − $62 = $3.50 to $2.50, which is $1.00 profit per unit.
