# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A drought cuts supply by 4% and demand is inelastic, with an elasticity of −0.2. By roughly how much must the price rise to clear the market?
   - 4%
   - 0.8%
   - [x] 20%
   - 200%
   > Price change ≈ quantity change / elasticity = 4% / 0.2 = 20%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] Oil demand is expected to rise 2 million barrels per day while supply rises 0.5 million barrels per day. What is the daily shortfall that inventories or prices must absorb?
   - [x] 1.5 million barrels per day
   - 2.5 million barrels per day
   - 1 million barrels per day
   - 0.5 million barrels per day
   > The market is short by 2.0 − 0.5 = 1.5 million barrels per day.
