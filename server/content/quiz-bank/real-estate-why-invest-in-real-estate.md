# Quiz

1. {#q1} Where do the returns from an income-producing property come from?
   - Only from changes in the property's value
   - [x] From rent as well as from any change in the property's value
   - Only from government payments
   - From the property's color
   > A property pays rent while it is held, and its value can also rise or fall.

2. {#q2} Why is real estate often seen as a hedge against inflation?
   - It never changes in value
   - Governments guarantee its price
   - [x] Rents and property values have tended to rise along with the general price level over long periods
   - It is exempt from taxes
   > Landlords can raise rents as costs rise, so income and values have tended to follow inflation, though not always.

3. {#q3} Why can real estate help diversify a portfolio of stocks and bonds?
   - It always rises when stocks fall
   - It has no risk
   - [x] Its returns depend on local supply, demand, rents, and rates, so they don't move in lockstep with stocks and bonds
   - It is traded on the same exchange as stocks
   > Different drivers mean imperfect correlation, which can reduce overall portfolio swings.

4. {#q4} Which is a downside of owning property compared with stocks?
   - It can be sold instantly at no cost
   - [x] It is costly to trade and can take months to sell
   - It is never affected by interest rates
   - It requires no management
   > Real estate is illiquid, with high transaction costs and time needed to sell.

5. {#q5} How can an investor get real estate income without buying a building directly?
   - By buying a bond
   - [x] Through a real estate investment trust that holds properties and pays out most of its rental income
   - By holding cash
   - It is not possible
   > A REIT lets an investor own a share of a property portfolio and receive its distributed income.

6. {#calc1} [calc] A REIT pays $6,000 a year and the payout grows 3% a year as rents rise. About how much is the annual payout after 5 years?
   - About $6,900
   - About $6,180
   - [x] About $6,956
   - About $7,200
   > $6,000 × 1.03^5 ≈ $6,956.

7. {#bk1} [calc] A property yields 5% in net rent and its value rises 3% in the year. What is the total return?
   - 15%
   - [x] 8%
   - 2%
   - 5%
   > Total return ≈ income yield + appreciation = 5% + 3% = 8%.
