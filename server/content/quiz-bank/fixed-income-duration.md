# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A bond priced at $980 has a modified duration of 7.2. If its yield rises 50 basis points, what is the approximate price change?
   - $35.28
   - $3.60
   - [x] −$35.28
   - −$0.04
   > Price change ≈ −modified duration × yield change × price = −7.2 × 0.0050 × $980 = −$35.28.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A portfolio worth $5 million has a modified duration of 6.0. What is its DV01, the dollar change for a one-basis-point move in yield?
   - $30,000
   - [x] $3,000
   - $300
   - $0
   > DV01 = duration × value × 0.0001 = 6.0 × $5,000,000 × 0.0001 = $3,000.
