# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A person wants to deposit $85,000 in cash in separate deposits, each below the $10,000 reporting level. What is the smallest number of deposits needed?
   - [x] 9
   - 8
   - 10
   - 85
   > Each deposit is at most $9,999. $85,000 / $9,999 = 8.5, so 9 deposits are needed.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A shop reports $100,000 of cash revenue, of which $30,000 is real sales and $70,000 is illegal cash passed through. What percentage of the reported revenue is illicit?
   - 30%
   - 100%
   - [x] 70%
   - 42.9%
   > $70,000 / $100,000 = 70%.
