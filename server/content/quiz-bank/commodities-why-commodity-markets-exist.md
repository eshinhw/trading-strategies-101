# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] An airline needs 2 million gallons of fuel and hedges at $2.90 a gallon. Fuel later trades at $3.30. How much does the hedge save compared with buying at the market?
   - $80,000
   - $8,000,000
   - [x] $800,000
   - $400,000
   > The airline pays $2.90 instead of $3.30, saving $0.40 × 2,000,000 = $800,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A wheat farmer sells 20,000 bushels forward at $7.00. At harvest wheat trades at $6.20. How much more does the farmer receive than the market price?
   - [x] $16,000
   - $1,600
   - $140,000
   - $124,000
   > The farmer receives $7.00 instead of $6.20, a gain of $0.80 × 20,000 = $16,000.
