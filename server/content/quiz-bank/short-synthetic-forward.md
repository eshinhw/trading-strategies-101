# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A trader builds a Short Synthetic Forward: buys one $100 put and sells one $100 call, for no net premium. What is the maximum profit at expiration, per share?
   - $0
   - [x] $100
   - $101
   - $99
   > For this position (K = 100), the maximum profit is $100 per share. Formula: P_max = K + netCF.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader builds a Short Synthetic Forward: buys one $100 put and sells one $100 call, for no net premium. What is the maximum profit on 10 contracts (100 shares each), in dollars?
   - $1,000
   - $10,000
   - $110,000
   - [x] $100,000
   > The maximum profit is $100 per share. One contract covers 100 shares, so 10 contracts give $100 × 100 × 10 = $100,000.
