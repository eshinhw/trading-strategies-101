# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] Using a Taylor rule, policy rate = neutral real rate (2%) + inflation + 0.5 × (inflation − target) + 0.5 × output gap, what rate is suggested with inflation at 4%, a 2% target, and an output gap of -1%?
   - 6%
   - 7.5%
   - 5%
   - [x] 6.5%
   > Rate = 2 + 4 + 0.5 × (4 − 2) + 0.5 × (−1) = 6.5%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] Using a Taylor rule, policy rate = neutral real rate (2%) + inflation + 0.5 × (inflation − target) + 0.5 × output gap, what rate is suggested with inflation at 3%, a 2% target, and an output gap of 1%?
   - 5%
   - [x] 6%
   - 7%
   - 4.5%
   > Rate = 2 + 3 + 0.5 × (3 − 2) + 0.5 × (1) = 6%.

3. {#bk3} [calc] The central bank raises its policy rate from 4.50% to 5.25%. How many basis points is the increase?
   - 7.5 basis points
   - 0.75 basis points
   - 750 basis points
   - [x] 75 basis points
   > The change is 0.75 percentage points, which is 75 basis points.
