# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A barbell holds 40% in a bond with duration 2 and 60% in a bond with duration 12. What is the portfolio duration?
   - 7 years
   - 6 years
   - 1.7 years
   - [x] 8 years
   > Portfolio duration is the weighted average: 0.4 × 2 + 0.6 × 12 = 8 years.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A barbell holds 30% in a bond with duration 3 and 70% in a bond with duration 15. What is the portfolio duration?
   - 9 years
   - [x] 11.4 years
   - 6.6 years
   - 2.5 years
   > Portfolio duration is the weighted average: 0.3 × 3 + 0.7 × 15 = 11.4 years.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A barbell puts 50% in a 2-year bond yielding 3.5% and 50% in a 10-year bond yielding 4.5%. What is the portfolio yield?
   - 3.5%
   - 4.5%
   - 8.0%
   - [x] 4.0%
   > Yield = 0.5 × 3.5% + 0.5 × 4.5% = 4.0%.
