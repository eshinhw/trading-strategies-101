# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A call option has a delta of 0.55. If the stock rises $2, by about how much does the option price change?
   - +$2.00
   - +$0.55
   - +$3.64
   - [x] +$1.10
   > Option change ≈ delta × stock change = 0.55 × $2 = $1.10.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A put option has a delta of −0.40. If the stock falls $3, by about how much does the put price change?
   - −$1.20
   - [x] +$1.20
   - +$3.00
   - +$7.50
   > Put change ≈ −0.40 × (−$3) = +$1.20. A put gains when the stock falls.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A trader is short 10 call contracts (100 shares each) with a delta of 0.45. How many shares should be bought to be delta-neutral?
   - 1,000 shares
   - 45 shares
   - 550 shares
   - [x] 450 shares
   > The short calls have delta −0.45 × 1,000 = −450. Buying 450 shares offsets it.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A trader owns 20 call contracts (100 shares each) with a delta of 0.60 and is short 500 shares of the stock. What is the net position delta in shares?
   - [x] +700
   - +1,200
   - −500
   - +200
   > The calls give 20 × 100 × 0.60 = +1,200 shares of delta. Adding −500 for the short stock gives +700.
