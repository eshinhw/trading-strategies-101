# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A stock is $100, a 1-year call with a $100 strike costs $10.45, and the interest rate is 5% (continuously compounded). By put-call parity, what is the 1-year put with the same strike worth?
   - $10.45
   - [x] $5.57
   - $15.57
   - $0.45
   > Put = call − stock + strike × e^(−rT) = $10.45 − $100 + $100 × e^(−0.05) = $5.57.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A stock is $50 and the strike is $48 with 6 months to expiry and a 4% continuously compounded rate. By put-call parity, what should call price minus put price equal?
   - $2.00
   - $50.00
   - $4.00
   - [x] $2.95
   > Call − put = stock − strike × e^(−rT) = $50 − $48 × e^(−0.02) = $2.95.
