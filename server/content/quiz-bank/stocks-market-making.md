# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A market maker quotes $20.00 bid and $20.04 ask and trades 10,000 shares on each side during the day, buying at the bid and selling at the ask. What is the gross spread income?
   - $800
   - $200
   - [x] $400
   - $4
   > Each round trip of a share earns $0.04, so 10,000 × $0.04 = $400.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A market maker buys 2,200 shares at the bid and sells 2,200 shares at the ask, with a $0.04 spread, and also ends the day with 800 extra shares bought at the bid. The price then falls $0.15. What is the net result (spread earned on matched trades minus inventory loss)?
   - [x] −$32
   - +$88
   - −$120
   - +$208
   > Matched round trips earn 2,200 × $0.04 = $88. The extra 800 shares lose 800 × $0.15 = $120. Net = $88 − $120 = −$32.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A market maker's quote is $20.00 bid and $20.04 ask. After buying more than selling, they hold 500 extra shares and shift both quotes down by $0.02 to encourage buyers. What are the new quotes?
   - $20.02 bid and $20.06 ask
   - $19.98 bid and $20.04 ask
   - [x] $19.98 bid and $20.02 ask
   - $20.00 bid and $20.02 ask
   > Shifting both sides down by $0.02 keeps the $0.04 spread: bid $19.98 and ask $20.02.
