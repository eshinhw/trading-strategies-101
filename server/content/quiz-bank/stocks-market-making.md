# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A market maker quotes $20.00 bid and $20.04 ask and trades 10,000 shares on each side during the day, buying at the bid and selling at the ask. What is the gross spread income?
   - $800
   - $200
   - [x] $400
   - $4
   > Each round trip of a share earns $0.04, so 10,000 × $0.04 = $400.
