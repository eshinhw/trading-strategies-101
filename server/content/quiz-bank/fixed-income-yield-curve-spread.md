# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] The 10-year yield is 4.20% and the 2-year yield is 3.80%. What is the 2s10s spread in basis points?
   - 400 basis points
   - 8.0 basis points
   - 0.4 basis points
   - [x] 40 basis points
   > 4.20% − 3.80% = 0.40%, which is 40 basis points.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A trader holds a duration-neutral curve steepener with a DV01 of $8,000 per basis point of spread. The 2s10s spread widens from 40 to 55 basis points. What is the gain?
   - $8,000
   - [x] $120,000
   - $12,000
   - $320,000
   > The spread widens 15 basis points, so the gain is $8,000 × 15 = $120,000.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A flattener loses when the curve steepens. A flattener has a DV01 of $6,500 per basis point of spread and the spread moves from 52 to 60 basis points. What is the result?
   - +$52,000
   - −$6,500
   - +$8,000
   - [x] −$52,000
   > The spread widened by 8 basis points against the flattener: −$6,500 × 8 = −$52,000.
