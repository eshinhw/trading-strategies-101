# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A building produces net operating income of $60,000 a year. Similar buildings sell at a 6% cap rate. What is the building's estimated value?
   - $3,600
   - [x] $1,000,000
   - $1,200,000
   - $800,000
   > Value = net operating income / cap rate = $60,000 / 0.06 = $1,000,000.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A building produces net operating income of $75,000 a year. Similar buildings sell at a 5% cap rate. What is the building's estimated value?
   - $3,750
   - $1,800,000
   - $1,200,000
   - [x] $1,500,000
   > Value = net operating income / cap rate = $75,000 / 0.05 = $1,500,000.

3. {#bk3} [calc] A building produces net operating income of $90,000 a year. Similar buildings sell at a 7.5% cap rate. What is the building's estimated value?
   - $6,750
   - [x] $1,200,000
   - $1,440,000
   - $960,000
   > Value = net operating income / cap rate = $90,000 / 0.075 = $1,200,000.

4. {#bk4} [calc] An apartment building collects $120,000 in rent and pays $45,000 in operating expenses. It sells for $1,250,000. What is the cap rate?
   - 9.6%
   - 3.6%
   - [x] 6%
   - 10%
   > NOI = $120,000 − $45,000 = $75,000. Cap rate = $75,000 / $1,250,000 = 6%.

5. {#bk5} [calc] A property sells for $900,000 and earns $90,000 in annual gross rent. What is the gross rent multiplier?
   - [x] 10
   - 0.1
   - 90
   - 8.1
   > Gross rent multiplier = price / annual gross rent = $900,000 / $90,000 = 10.

6. {#bk6} [calc] A building's NOI is $80,000 and the cap rate rises from 5% to 6.4% because interest rates increased. Approximately how much does the value fall?
   - $250,000
   - $150,000
   - [x] $350,000
   - $1,250,000
   > Value at 5% is $80,000 / 0.05 = $1,600,000. Value at 6.4% is $80,000 / 0.064 = $1,250,000. The fall is $350,000.
