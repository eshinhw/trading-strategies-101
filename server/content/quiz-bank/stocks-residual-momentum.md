# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A stock returned 15% over the past year, its beta to the market is 1.3, and the market returned 8%. What is its residual return?
   - 7.0%
   - 10.4%
   - [x] 4.6%
   - 1.4%
   > Market-explained return = 1.3 × 8% = 10.4%. Residual = 15% − 10.4% = 4.6%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A stock with a beta of 0.8 returned 6% while the market returned 10%. What is its residual return?
   - [x] −2%
   - +2%
   - −4%
   - +6%
   > Market-explained return = 0.8 × 10% = 8%. Residual = 6% − 8% = −2%.

3. {#bk3} [calc] Stock X has a raw return of 20% and a residual return of 3%. Stock Y has a raw return of 12% and a residual return of 9%. A residual momentum strategy ranks by residual return. Which stock ranks higher?
   - Stock X, because its raw return is higher
   - They tie, since the average is 16%
   - [x] Stock Y, because its residual return is higher
   - Stock X, because 20% is above 12%
   > Residual momentum ignores the part explained by the market, so Y's 9% beats X's 3%.

4. {#bk4} [calc] A stock's raw 12-month return is 22%, its beta is 1.2 and the market returned 15%. What is the residual return, ignoring any intercept?
   - 7%
   - [x] 4%
   - 18%
   - 22%
   > Market-explained return = 1.2 × 15% = 18%. Residual = 22% − 18% = 4%.

5. {#bk5} [calc] Two stocks each returned 20%. Stock X has a beta of 1.5 and Stock Y a beta of 0.5. The market returned 10%. Which has the higher residual return, and by how much?
   - Stock X, by 10 percentage points
   - Stock Y, by 5 percentage points
   - They are equal
   - [x] Stock Y, by 10 percentage points
   > X's residual = 20% − 15% = 5%. Y's residual = 20% − 5% = 15%. Y is higher by 10 points.

6. {#bk6} Why can residual momentum be a cleaner signal than raw-return momentum?
   - It adds extra market exposure
   - [x] It removes the part of a stock's return explained by market, sector and other common factors
   - It ranks stocks by trading volume
   - It ignores the stock's returns altogether
   > Raw momentum can just reflect a stock being in a hot sector. The residual isolates the stock-specific trend.
