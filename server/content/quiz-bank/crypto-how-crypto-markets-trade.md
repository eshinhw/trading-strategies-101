2. {#bk2} [calc] An exchange charges a 0.10% taker fee on each trade. What is the total fee for buying and then selling $10,000 of a coin?
   - $10
   - $2
   - [x] $20
   - $200
   > Each trade costs 0.10% × $10,000 = $10. Two trades cost $20.
