# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A country's growth has been revised up from 2.0% to 2.8% while its peers are unchanged. By how much is growth momentum improved?
   - 0.4 percentage points
   - [x] 0.8 percentage points
   - 40%
   - 2.8 percentage points
   > The revision is 2.8% − 2.0% = 0.8 percentage points.

2. {#bk2} [calc] A macro fund adds $2 million of position for each 0.25 points an economy's GDP forecast is revised up. The forecast is raised from 2.4% to 2.9%. What position is added?
   - $2 million
   - $8 million
   - $1 million
   - [x] $4 million
   > The revision is 0.5 points, or two 0.25 steps. 2 × $2 million = $4 million.
