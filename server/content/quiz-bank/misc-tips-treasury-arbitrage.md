# Quiz

1. {#bk1} [calc] A TIPS yields 1.9% in real terms and a Treasury of the same maturity yields 4.1%. An inflation swap fixed rate is 2.0%. What is the arbitrage difference between the breakeven and the swap rate?
   - 2.2%
   - 0.1%
   - [x] 0.2%
   - 2.0%
   > Breakeven = 4.1% − 1.9% = 2.2%. Compared with the 2.0% swap, the difference is 0.2%.

2. {#bk2} [calc] On a $100 million position, a 0.2% annual mispricing between TIPS breakeven and the inflation swap rate is captured. What is the annual profit?
   - [x] $200,000
   - $2,000,000
   - $20,000
   - $100,000
   > Profit = 0.2% × $100 million = $200,000.
