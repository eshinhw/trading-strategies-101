## What Money Does

Money does three jobs. It is a medium of exchange, so people don't have to find someone who wants exactly what they have to trade, it is a store of value, so purchasing power can be saved for later, and it is a unit of account, so prices can be compared in a single measure. Without money, every transaction would require a direct swap of goods, which is slow and impractical.

## Uneven Cash Needs

Cash comes in and goes out at different times. A company may be paid by customers in March but not owe its suppliers until June, a bank has to be ready to meet withdrawals at any moment, and a government collects taxes in bursts while spending continuously. As a result, at any moment some organizations hold more cash than they need and others are short of it.

## Why Short-Term Cash Markets Exist

Short-term cash markets connect the two groups. Those with surplus cash want somewhere safe to earn a modest return for a few days or months, while those who are short want to borrow quickly and cheaply. Markets for instruments like Treasury bills, commercial paper, and repurchase agreements exist to match them, and those instruments are covered in the lessons that follow.

## Safety and Liquidity Come First

When an organization lends or invests spare cash, its first priorities are not losing the principal and being able to get the money back when needed, so yield comes third. This is why cash-like investments tend to be very short-term and issued by borrowers considered very safe. Giving up some return in exchange for safety and easy access is the trade-off that defines this part of the market.

## In Practice

A manufacturer receives 10 million dollars from a large customer in March but doesn't need to pay its suppliers until June. Rather than leave the money idle, it buys short-term Treasury bills that mature just before the payments are due, earning a small return with almost no risk. At the same time, a bank that needs extra funds overnight borrows cash from lenders in the money market, and both sides get what they need.
